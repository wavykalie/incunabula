# PROMOTIONS — the only channel between the voices

A ductus reads only its own ledger. When a preference earned by one voice turns out to
hold for another, it does not get copied: it is merged here, dated, with the evidence
named — and until that exists, the second voice does not hold it.

This is the rule the root ledger keeps between channels (`SELF_IMPROVEMENT.md` §2),
applied one layer down: evidence from one surface may not move another. `ductus.py
cross` reports an entry two ledgers both carry, because a preference held by both has
stopped being either one's. A merge recorded here is the only sanctioned crossing, and
it is the author's to write.

No merges have been made.
