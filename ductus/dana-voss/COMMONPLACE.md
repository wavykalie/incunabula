# COMMONPLACE - dana-voss

This voice's own taste, recorded as it is decided. A decision is dated and stands until a
later one contradicts it. A preference is written only after three agreeing decisions, is
marked `emergent`, and always carries a `limit:` line, because a voice that accretes rules
turns into a caricature of its first book.

The entry types are the root `COMMONPLACE.md`'s: `decision`, `preference`, `outcome`,
`promotion`. Nothing outside this directory is evidence for a dana-voss decision, and
nothing here is enforced against a book.

## Decisions

### 2026-11-12 - decision - the voice is authored, not inherited

The book was drafted under kalie-kirch, a literary-horror hand. Its refusals were kept as
binding while its pace was argued with, and the result read as a horror book with faster
sentences. This voice starts from the action register and takes nothing from the old hand
except the material.

- **limit:** the book's premise, world and cast are unchanged; only the hand is new.

### 2026-11-12 - decision - pace is the lane's, not the book's better pages

Mean sentence <= 13, short sentences >= 40%, paragraph <= 32, taken from `action-voice`
(12.8 / 42.9% / 31.7) rather than from the draft's best chapters, which is how the last
contract set a throttle the rest of the book could not hold.

- **limit:** a chapter may sit anywhere in the fold (9-14 mean); the book, not the chapter,
  is measured against the mean.

### 2026-11-12 - decision - measurement is pounds and paces

No metric measure on any page, in narration or dialogue or a quoted document. Weights are
what a back carries, heights are what a man falls, distances are paces to a door.

- **limit:** this is a diction rule, not a conversion pass; nothing is converted from the
  archived draft, because the sentence is rewritten either way.

### 2026-11-12 - decision - the goods catalogue is out

A coat is a coat. A trade word is spoken once by a man in that trade and never becomes the
narrator's default noun.

- **limit:** the city's own nouns (*vell*, *sare*, *vesh*, *hess*, *hoy*, *smork*) stay,
  one per paragraph, learned in the sentence.

### 2026-11-12 - decision - the draft is retired whole, not revised in place

The 13-chapter structure, the 30,759-word chapter 3 and the 15,900-word flashback chapter
are the old shape and are not carried over. The draft is archived word for word and is
evidence of what not to do, not a base to edit.

- **limit:** the club, the barkeep, the cellar caretaker and the runner survive as canon for
  the rewrite.

### 2026-11-12 - decision - the gore cap is withdrawn

The previous rule was that gore costs him something or happens off the page. The author
withdrew it: violence is written at the scale it happens, in the room, with the blood and
the smell and the weight, and the camera does not cut away to spare the reader.

- **limit:** the daughter's rape stays aftermath only and is never staged; no staged sexual
  violence; no staged animal cruelty. Those protect a person, not a reader's comfort.
