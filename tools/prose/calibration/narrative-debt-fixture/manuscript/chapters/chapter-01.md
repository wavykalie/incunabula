The alarm cycled and Kaname braced against the frame. The pain came up like chewing on
copper foil while someone struck a bronze bell inside her sinuses, and the console read
eight gravities across the nozzle skirt.

Kaname said, "Seal it, now."

Sakuya whispered, "Sealing. Give me four seconds."

The load gauge climbed past sixty thousand tons of drag on a tow line that was never
rated for the pull, and the deck plate under them answered like a struck bell. The
manifest listed fifty canisters of coolant in the bay.
