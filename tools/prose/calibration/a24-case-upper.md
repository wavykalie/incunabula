# A24 case pin - sentence-initial coda (ERRATA.md 2026-10-08)

The order holds the evening together. A tired household runs on structure, not
on improvisation, and the structure is what the child reads.

Parents ask why a fixed sequence works when willpower fails. This is why the
order holds: it removes the decision from the hour when nobody has a decision
left to make.

The fix is small and it is the same fix in every household. Decide the order
before the evening starts.
