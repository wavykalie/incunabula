# Chapter 34: City Eleven: Philadelphia

The Trocadero Theatre in Chinatown was our eleventh stop.

The historic Victorian hall was packed to the iron rafters with eight hundred people, its ornate gilded balconies vibrating with the raw, volatile energy of a Philadelphia crowd.

Julian was electric.

He took the stage with the swagger of a champion entering the championship round. His signs were so crisp, so lightning-fast and devastatingly funny that the room was in continuous, rolling hysteria from his opening beat.

At minute seventy-two, Julian stopped in the center of the stage.

He did not walk to the wings for his bow.

He stood in the bright circle of the spotlight, raised his hands, and pointed directly at me where I stood in my cream sweater on stage left.

The room went quiet.

*For thirty-five days,* Julian signed, his face serious, dignified, and luminous with pride, *hearing people have asked me how a deaf comic performs on tour. They look at Maya standing beside me, and they call her my interpreter.*

I voiced his words, my throat tight with emotion, my voice ringing clear through the theater sound system.

Julian took three deliberate steps across the apron, stopped beside me, and took my right hand in his.

*She is not my interpreter,* Julian signed to the eight hundred people in the Trocadero. *Her name is Maya Lin. She is my artistic co-creator, my equal partner, and the love of my life.*

The theater didn't just applaud.

The entire audience rose in a thunderous standing ovation. Deaf patrons waved their hands high in the air; hearing patrons screamed and cheered, stamping their feet until the floorboards roared.

Julian raised our joined hands high above our heads, bowing with me as the Philadelphia crowd showered us with love."""
