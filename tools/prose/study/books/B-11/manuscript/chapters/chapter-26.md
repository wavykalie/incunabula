# Chapter 26: The Investigation

**MEMORANDUM — URGENT & CONFIDENTIAL**
*To: Senior Leadership Team & Board of Governors*
*From: D. Henderson (Head of Year 8 / Acting Safety Officer)*
*Date: Friday, 18 October 2024 (11:30 AM)*
*Subject: Catastrophic Structural Fixture Failure — West Stairwell*

**Executive Overview:**
At approximately 08:45 AM today, an industrial fire safety appliance (Type 4 Water-Gas Extinguisher, 10-litre capacity, approximate gross weight 24.5 kg) detached from its wall mounting bracket on the upper landing of the West Wing service stairs.

The vessel descended twenty-four concrete steps, resulting in major structural damage to stair treads 3, 7, and 12, puncture damage to the ground-floor fire exit doors, and secondary discharge of pressurized contents across the lower corridor.

By extraordinary fortune, catastrophic student fatality was avoided. Pupil Rimmer, Chloe (Form 8A) sustained minor facial lacerations and severe shock when she was forcibly pushed clear of the projectile's trajectory by pupil Bell, Sadie (Form 8H). Both pupils were treated by the school nurse and subsequently collected by guardians.

**Premises Inspection Findings (Joint Report with Health & Safety Executive Inspector):**
1. The wall mounting bracket (fabricated cast iron, 1972 specification) failed due to sudden mechanical shear across the primary retaining collar.
2. Metallurgical inspection of the fractured surfaces revealed no gradual fatigue oxidation. The shear plane presents as clean, instantaneous ductile rupture, consistent with extraordinary dynamic shock or localized structural torsion.
3. Wall anchor bolts remained seated within the cinderblock masonry; the failure occurred entirely within the solid cast-iron bracket body.
4. Mr. Garside (Premises Manager) notes that this fixture passed scheduled statutory inspection during the August maintenance interval with zero defects recorded.

**CCTV Footage Analysis (Camera C-07 — West Stairwell Upper):**
The undersigned reviewed high-resolution digital playback of the 60-second window preceding the detachment (08:44:00 to 08:45:00).

- 08:44:30: High-density student transit. Pupil Rimmer descends accompanied by peers Mills and Vance.
- 08:44:48: Pupil Bell ascends rapidly against the traffic flow, vocalizing warnings.
- 08:44:51: *Anomalous optical artifact.* The footage displays a sudden localized drop in exposure value around the mounting bracket, accompanied by severe digital noise banding.
- 08:44:52: The bracket fractures. No human contact with the extinguisher is recorded at the moment of failure. The nearest individual (Pupil Rimmer) was approximately four feet distant from the wall mount.

*Inspector's Preliminary Observation:* The HSE representative stated unofficially that the bracket appeared to experience "concentrated torsional stress as if levered by a hydraulic tool," despite the total absence of any physical lever or tampering in the video record.

**Emergency Measures Enacted:**
1. Immediate, total closure of the West Stairwell and adjacent service corridors under Section 4 Health & Safety at Work Act 1974.
2. Structural survey commissioned across all 1972 extension masonry to assess secondary vibration damage.
3. Commendation flagged for pupil Bell, Sadie for rapid situational awareness and life-saving intervention.
4. Year 8 assembly scheduled for Monday morning to address student anxiety and persistent rumors regarding building integrity.

**Addendum to Pastoral Memo — Structural Health & Safety HSE Investigation:**
At 13:00 PM, an engineering surveyor from the Doncaster Borough Council (Mr. K. O'Connor) conducted an initial seismic rebound test on the load-bearing cinderblock masonry of the west stairwell.

The test results indicate an anomalous deflection of 3.2 mm across the vertical axis of the wall, with secondary fracture lines extending downward into the foundation footing of Boiler Room Three. Mr. O'Connor noted that the cinderblock shows evidence of localized compressive stress consistent with subsurface ground subsidence.

A formal prohibition notice has been issued under Section 22 of the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974, prohibiting all student access to the West Wing until permanent steel structural shoring can be installed.

*Signed: D. Henderson (Pastoral) / K. O'Connor (HSE Surveyor)*

**Addendum to HSE Incident Review — Structural Safety Log:**
At 14:30 PM, an emergency conference call was convened between the Headteacher (Mr. R. Vance), the Chair of Governors (Mrs. H. Armitage), and the Chief Building Surveyor for the South Yorkshire Education Authority.

The surveyor confirmed that ground-penetrating radar scans conducted beneath the west wing have revealed an active void approximately fourteen feet below the foundation raft, corresponding to the abandoned ventilation drift of the Ashfield Main seam.

A preliminary recommendation has been issued for the phased evacuation of the entire school site if structural settlement exceeds 5.0 mm over the forthcoming weekend.

*Recorded by: D. Henderson (Acting Safety Officer)*

**Addendum to Pastoral Memo — Premises Structural Survey:**
At 15:30 PM, an emergency structural inspection was carried out on the load-bearing cinderblock masonry of the west stairwell by structural engineers from the South Yorkshire Metropolitan Authority.

The inspection team identified three vertical shear fractures running from the first-floor landing down to the foundation footing of Boiler Room Three. The senior engineer reported that the wall had experienced an anomalous torsional shock of unknown origin, resulting in a 4.1 mm outward displacement of the cinderblock facade.

A statutory prohibition notice has been posted on the double fire doors, and heavy timber raking shores have been erected across the stair landings to prevent progressive collapse of the floor slabs.

*Signed: D. Henderson (Pastoral) / M. Lockwood (Structural Engineer)*

*Date: Friday, 18 October 2024*

**Dear Parent / Guardian,**
We write to inform you that following an isolated mechanical fixture failure in the West Wing service stairwell this morning, the school has enacted precautionary safety protocols under the guidance of the South Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service and the Health and Safety Executive.

No students suffered serious injury during the incident. As a precautionary measure, the West Wing classrooms and gymnasium will remain closed for the remainder of the current week to facilitate an ultrasonic structural survey of the building foundations.
