# Chapter 13: The Behavioral Slip

**ASHFIELD COMPREHENSIVE SCHOOL**
*Incident & Maintenance Dispatch — Premises & Pastoral Joint Record*
*Date: Tuesday, 15 October 2024*
*Logged by: D. Henderson (Head of Year 8) & E. Garside (Premises Manager)*

**Incident Code:** VAND-441-GYM
**Location:** West Wing / Lower Gymnasium Corridor / Bay 4 / Locker 142
**Time of Event:** Discovered 11:22 AM during Period 3 transition

**Incident Summary:**
Pupil Rimmer, Chloe (Form 8A) reported an inability to access Locker 142 prior to physical education instruction. Visual and physical inspection of the security fixture (brass combination lock, personal property of pupil) indicated deliberate sabotage utilizing an industrial polymer bonding agent.

Premises Manager utilized mechanical bolt cutters (36-inch drop-forged) to sever the hardened shackle and gain entry to the locker compartment. Internal locker contents (PE kit, footwear, personal grooming sundries) remained intact and undamaged.

**Premises Manager Notes (E. Garside):**
The bonding agent used is 'Devcon 5-Minute Steel Weld' or equivalent two-part epoxy. This material is not available in student science laboratories or design technology stores. It is stocked exclusively in the central boiler room maintenance cage beneath the boiler house.

The lock on the boiler room service hatch was inspected at 11:45 AM. The hasp showed no signs of forced entry. However, the secondary access hatch behind Boiler Three was found unbolted from the inside. I have repeatedly advised the Senior Leadership Team that access to the 1972 pipe basements remains an ongoing hazard to structural and physical security.

**Pastoral Investigation & Suspect Assessment (D. Henderson):**
Pupil Rimmer was interviewed in the Year Office at 12:15 PM in a state of marked distress. Pupil alleged that she is being "targeted by jealous peers" and demanded that the entire Year 8 cohort have their bags searched.

The undersigned reviewed electronic corridor CCTV logs covering the lower gym wing between 08:30 and 11:15 AM.
- 08:30–09:00: Normal student ingress. High density. No individual observed tampering with Locker 142.
- 09:00–10:00 (Period 1): Corridor vacant. Cleaning staff (Mrs. Briggs) present with floor buffer.
- 10:00–10:15 (Morning Break): High density. Multiple pupils gathered near water fountain. No suspicious loitering recorded directly in front of Locker 142.
- 10:15–11:15 (Period 2): Corridor vacant. No motion triggers logged on camera sensor C-04.

*Conclusion:* The sabotage was either executed during the chaotic 15-minute morning break window with high technical efficiency, or the perpetrator gained access during unauthorized out-of-bounds intervals.

Suspicion currently centers on older male students associated with the Year 11 rugby cohort following an altercation involving pupil Rimmer's elder sibling at the Thorne fairgrounds on Saturday evening.

*Pupil Bell, Sadie (Form 8H)* was logged by corridor sweep at 11:25 AM standing near the fire exit. When questioned, pupil stated she was awaiting entry to the library. No connection to the incident is indicated; pupil possesses no history of aggressive or technical vandalism.

**Parental Communication Log (13:10 PM):**
Mrs. V. Rimmer (mother of Chloe) contacted main school reception via telephone demanding an immediate interview with the Headteacher. Mrs. Rimmer stated that Chloe is "deeply traumatised by this targeted assault on her privacy" and asserted that Ashfield Comprehensive is failing in its statutory duty of care.

The undersigned took the call and attempted to de-escalate. Mrs. Rimmer refused pastoral mediation and threatened to escalate the matter to the Board of Governors unless the perpetrator is excluded before the end of the current school week.

**Confidential Pastoral Observation (Internal Year Office Only):**
The social hierarchy within Year 8 has shown increasing instability since the beginning of the autumn term. Pupil Rimmer occupies an informal leadership position characterised by coercive peer dynamics, particularly targeting quieter female students in Form 8H and Form 8C.

While vandalism of school property cannot be condoned under any circumstances, pastoral staff should be mindful that retaliatory actions of this nature rarely emerge in isolation. The Year Head recommends an informal review of peer relationships within Form 8A prior to the half-term break.

**Corrective Actions:**
1. Replacement padlock issued to pupil Rimmer from emergency school stock (brass key model, deposit £3.50 collected).
2. Warning notice posted on Year 8 bulletin board regarding malicious damage to school fixtures (Sanction: Level 4 Exclusion).
3. Mr. Garside requested to double-check boiler room security padlocks before 16:00 daily.
4. Electronic incident entry committed to the local behavioral tracking database under Category B (Property Damage).

**Addendum to Maintenance Dispatch — Premises Manager Log:**
At 14:15 PM, the undersigned inspected the foundation drainage sump beneath the west boiler house. Water levels in the inspection pit have risen four inches since Monday morning, carrying a heavy suspension of dark coal silt and sulfurous mud.

The sump pump operated for twelve minutes under manual override before tripping the circuit breaker on Distribution Board B. Upon removing the cast-iron pump strainer, I discovered a two-inch piece of mineralized shale wedged inside the impeller housing. The shale displays fresh fracture facets consistent with recent tectonic or structural shifting in the underlying strata.

I have notified the District Maintenance Surveyor (Mr. T. Braithwaite) that the foundation slab requires ultrasonic core testing before the winter frosts set in.

*Signed: E. Garside (Premises Manager)*
