# Chapter 8: The Sunken Highway

The Toyota Land Cruiser cruised north along Highway 83 at eighty-five kilometers an hour, the tires humming on the bitumen. The road cut through the red plains like a black pencil line drawn across a rusted iron plate, straight and unbroken for forty miles between irrigation settlements.

On either side of the asphalt, the paddocks were empty. The dead sunflowers bowed their dry, blackened heads toward the cracked earth, their stalks like brittle stakes driven into the dust. Every five miles, an abandoned homestead stood behind a row of dead peppercorn trees, windows boarded up with plywood, windmills locked with wire to keep the blades from spinning themselves to pieces in the dry westerlies.

Evelyn reached over and pressed the record button on the Olympus digital voice recorder clipped to her sun visor.

*Brand, field notes. Tuesday, November fourth, fourteen-fifteen hours.*

She paused, watching a heat mirage shimmer across the road half a mile ahead, turning an oncoming grain truck into a floating, silver hovercraft.

*Re-inspection of Mercer site completed. Physical markers confirm identical signature to Miller site, Merriwa, and Oakhaven Winery, 2021. Primary characteristics: one, forty-two degree inverted thermal cone against primary masonry structure. Two, localized aggregate spalling on concrete foundation slab exceeding six hundred degrees within initial ten minutes of combustion. Three, absence of volatile petroleum distillates. Four, presence of engineered low-level ventilation draft using foundation crawlspace flues.*

She checked her rearview mirror. The road behind her was completely empty, a ribbon of heat-distorted bitumen fading into the dust haze.

*Underwriting analysis reveals common corporate nexus. All three properties encumbered by second mortgages to Murrumbidgee Rural Finance, subsidiary of Apex Agro-Holdings. In all three instances, mortgagor held un-digitized, senior historical water rights under pre-nineteen fifteen Crown allocations. Arson objective is not property insurance payout; property insurance payout is the collateral mechanism to clear mortgage default while senior water entitlement transfers to corporate holding trust.*

She clicked the recorder off.

The dashboard thermometer crept from forty-two to forty-three degrees.

She pulled the Land Cruiser off the blacktop into a gravel truck bay beside a rusted cattle ramp. The tires crunched across dry red pebbles. She turned off the engine, stepped down into the blinding heat, and walked around the vehicle to check the tire pressures with her brass dial gauge. Thirty-six pounds on all four corners.

She knelt beside the front left tire and pulled her steel awl from her belt. Two small quartz chips were wedged into the tread. She pried them out with clean, deliberate movements, watching them drop into the gravel.

She pulled her notebook onto the steering wheel at the roadside rest stop and began drafting the executive memorandum that would challenge the regional management.

*TO: Warren Holst, Regional Managing Director.*
*FROM: Evelyn Brand, Senior Fire Claims Assessor.*
*DATE: 04 November 2026.*
*SUBJECT: CLM-2026-8841-MERCER / Formal Notice of Suspected Incendiary Origin.*

*1. Physical evidence recovered from the Mercer homestead site confirms non-accidental combustion using a high-density synthetic chemical accelerant.*
*2. Thermal geometry and forensic markers demonstrate identical physical signatures to Claim NM-CLM-2021-0419 (Oakhaven Winery) and Claim NM-CLM-2026-7712 (Miller Homestead).*
*3. In all three instances, the insured structures were encumbered by second mortgages held by financing entities controlled by Apex Agro-Holdings, with mandatory loss-payee covenants attached to pre-1915 Crown water allocations.*
*4. Immediate suspension of claims settlement is required pursuant to Section 54 of the Insurance Contracts Act, pending formal referral to the State Police Arson Squad and the Australian Securities and Investments Commission.*

She read the words twice in the dim light of the cab.

Once this document was lodged in the central system, it became part of the permanent corporate record. It could not be deleted by an administrative clerk. It could not be buried by an executive committee without exposing the directors to criminal liability for concealment of a felony.

Ahead of her, thirty miles to the northeast, the silos of Wagga Wagga would appear above the river gums within the hour.

Warren Holst would be waiting in his corner office on the fourth floor of the National Mutual building, with his polished tortoiseshell glasses, his green leather blotter, and his corporate assurances about fiduciary duty and client relationships.

She shifted her hands on the steering wheel, her knuckles cracking in the quiet cab.

Beside the cattle grid, a white plastic marker post stood in the dirt, bearing the blue enamel crest of the Murrumbidgee Irrigation Commission. Bolted to the timber frame behind it was an electronic telemetry transmitter, its solar panel covered in a thick layer of red dust.

Evelyn walked over to the concrete irrigation ditch running parallel to the fence line. The concrete flume was completely dry, choked with dead tumbleweeds and bleached sheep bones. Five years ago, water had flowed through this ditch four days a week, feeding twenty small orchard blocks between here and the highway junction. Now, the main control gate was padlocked with a heavy steel chain, secured with a corporate seal bearing the initials of Apex Agro-Holdings.

She pulled her field notebook from her pocket and copied the identification serial number stamped into the brass collar of the water flow meter. Meter Number W-4491. Under the regional water sharing plan, that allocation had guaranteed four hundred megaliters of high-security water annually to the Mercer title. If the homestead was condemned and the mortgage defaulted, that allocation would be detached from the land and transferred directly to the corporate holding account in Griffith.

A dust devil spins across the blacktop ahead, scattering dry burrs against the radiator grille.
