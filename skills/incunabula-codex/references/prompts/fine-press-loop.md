# Phase 5 — Fine Press Loop

## Goal

Push the manuscript until its calibrated literary floor clears the target the user set,
using 8.5 as the default only when no other number was given.

The phase exists because a manuscript can pass every structural check and still not work as
literature. This loop aims at the ceiling: voice, residue, risk, contradiction, thematic
pressure, and what the reader carries out of the book.

## Read first

- `artifacts/08-devils-audit.md`
- every chapter
- the foundation and architecture artifacts
- the latest Incunabula Score report, if one exists
- earlier files in `evaluations/`, if this is not the first pass

## Discount before you believe a number

Self-assessment reads high. Before deciding the book cleared the bar:

1. Score every lens from 1 to 10.
2. Subtract 0.8 from each lens score unless there is outside human critique attached in
   `evaluations/`.
3. The lowest calibrated lens is the Fine Press score.

The threshold is cleared only when that calibrated floor is above the target. A high average
with one lens below the line is not a pass.

## The seven lenses

Score and diagnose each separately.

1. **Line** — sentence quality, rhythm, precision, image pressure, absence of generic polish.
2. **Voice** — recognisable, durable across pages, resistant to synthetic sameness.
3. **Character** — contradiction, memory, self-sabotage, how they behave under pressure.
4. **Structure** — chapter necessity, escalation, reversals, payoff, how it reads the second
   time.
5. **Theme** — inquiry that is lived rather than lectured, subtext, moral and intellectual
   pressure.
6. **Emotion** — feeling that was earned, the aftertaste, what the body does while reading.
7. **Originality** — premise, execution, angle, refusal of the genre's defaults.

## The loop

Repeat until the calibrated floor is above target or a blocker is named:

1. Identify the lowest lens.
2. Name the exact passages or structural zones holding the ceiling down.
3. Pick the smallest revision class that could raise it: structural, connective, character
   deepening, voice recalibration, line edit, or cut-and-merge.
4. Make the revision in the manuscript files themselves.
5. Write a revision note to `evaluations/fine-press-loop.md`.
6. Rescore every lens, including the ones the revision might have damaged.

## Revision rules

- Keep the strongest passages unless they are structurally false.
- Do not add decorative lyricism to chase a literary number.
- Concrete pressure beats abstract explanation.
- Scene behaviour beats thematic statement.
- One strange true detail beats three polished generic sentences.
- A chapter that cannot justify itself gets cut or merged.
- A character who behaves correctly too often needs cost, evasion, contradiction, or
  self-inflicted damage.
- Prose that is smooth and forgettable needs specificity, asymmetry, silence, interruption,
  or a harder image.

## When to stop

**Approve** only when the calibrated floor is above target, no lens sits below it, no phase 4
finding at major severity is still open, and the report cites text or structure rather than
impression.

**Block** when there is not enough draft to evaluate, when the next real improvement needs an
author decision or material from outside the project, or when further revision would be
speculation. Do not keep looping until an evaluator happens to agree with you.

## Output

`evaluations/fine-press-loop.md`:

- target threshold
- iteration number
- lens scores, raw and calibrated
- lowest lens
- revisions made
- passages changed
- strengths preserved
- verdict: `CONTINUE`, `APPROVED`, or `BLOCKED`
