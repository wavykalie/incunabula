# Premise forge (optional)

Run this before foundation work when the launch profile is selected, or whenever the
premise lacks a contradiction that can escalate on its own. The purpose is to strengthen
the author's idea, never to replace it.

## Inputs

The original idea, the research and comp-title map, the audience and positioning, and
whatever is known about reader frustrations or open market space.

## Produce five variants

1. The raw idea, scored honestly.
2. The protagonist or role inverted.
3. The stakes or the source of pressure relocated.
4. A fusion with the market gap.
5. A collapsed character contradiction.

Score each on hook, irony engine, native escalation, central question, gap fit, and
retellability — and take the lowest of those six, not the average.

## Choosing

Pick the strongest variant only after stating what changed and why. The author has to be
able to see their original idea inside the winner; if they cannot, the forge has failed
regardless of the numbers. Where the launch profile demands a floor of 8.0 and no variant
reaches it after one re-forge round, proceed with the best variant and flag the shortfall
rather than pretending it cleared.

## Output

Write the chosen premise, its contradiction, the escalation ladder, the central question,
and the selection rationale to `premise.md`. Keep every rejected variant and its scores in
the intake artifacts. Nothing is discarded silently.
