# Phase 2 — Architecture

## Goal

Turn the foundation into a structure that can be drafted with momentum. Architecture is
where the book stops being a set of good ideas and becomes a sequence of moves with
consequences.

## Outputs

- `artifacts/05-outline.md`
- `artifacts/07-opening-strategy.md`
- `PROJECT_STATE.yaml`

## Rules

- Outline in chapters or blocks, each with one clear narrative function.
- Name the turn that ends each major block. If a block ends where it began, it is not a block.
- Carry the escalation logic through from the story engine. Pressure should compound, not
  restart.
- Make the architecture agree with the emotional curve. Where they disagree, one of them is
  wrong and the version already on paper is not automatically the right one.
- Keep a tension map visible enough that a reader of the outline can see where the book
  tightens and where it deliberately lets go.
- The opening must promise what the book actually delivers. An opening that promises a
  different book is the most expensive mistake in this phase, because everything downstream
  inherits it.

## What goes in each file

`artifacts/05-outline.md` — macro structure, the chapter or block outline, the narrative
function of each, the central conflict, each block's ending turn, and the tension map.

`artifacts/07-opening-strategy.md` — the opening strategy, first-line options, the promise
the first page makes, and where chapter one ends.

## Opening quality

- The first line has to earn the second line. Nothing else.
- The opening must establish at least one of: voice, tension, or a reason to care.
- Chapter one's last line must increase forward pressure rather than release it.

## Before you finish

Maintenance gate: check out to state and canon. Re-run the architecture gate.
