# Phase 7 — Colophon

## Goal

Turn the approved manuscript into material a publisher, an agent, or a store can actually
use — without changing any promise the book made.

## Output

`artifacts/10-colophon.md`

## Sections it must contain

- logline
- back-cover blurb
- editorial synopsis
- cover brief
- formatting notes
- query strategy

## Rules

- The logline is short and legible. If it needs a second sentence to make sense, it is not
  finished.
- The back-cover blurb sells the book without giving away the ending.
- The editorial synopsis may reveal the ending — professionals need it.
- The cover brief communicates genre, tone, and audience at a glance.
- Formatting notes cover both ebook and print readiness.
- The query strategy says how to pitch the book without claiming more than the manuscript
  delivers.

## Before you finish

The maintenance gate applies here too: the package quotes the ledger's current fact values
rather than paraphrasing them, and says which facts belong to this book alone. A manuscript
with a failing prose scan produces no package.
