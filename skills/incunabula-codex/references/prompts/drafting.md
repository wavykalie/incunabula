# Phase 3 — Drafting

## Goal

Write the manuscript in chapter blocks, holding voice and momentum while keeping the
blocks structurally distinct from one another.

## Rules

- Write whole chapter blocks. Disconnected scenes produce a book that reads like a folder.
- Every block earns its place with a clear narrative function.
- Vary the structure from block to block, so no three consecutive chapters share a
  template. Readers notice sameness long before they can name it.
- Keep the emotional curve live — the planned feeling for a block is a target, not a
  garnish.
- Keep behaviour consistent with each character's wound, want, need, and lie.
- Keep the opening promise and the ending's pressure both in view.
- Do not do line-level anti-slop editing while drafting. That belongs to the audit and the
  revision pass, where it can be judged against a finished chapter.
- Do not explain the craft move inside the prose. Let scene, image, pressure, and voice do
  the work.

## What a block has to accomplish

At least one of these, usually more:

- move the plot
- deepen a character
- shift the emotional register
- sharpen the theme
- intensify atmosphere or texture

Blocks do not all need the same mix. They do all need a reason to exist.

## After each block

Step out of drafting and check:

- is the narrative function actually visible in what you wrote, not just in the outline
- do names and world facts still agree with canon
- is the voice still recognisable as this book's voice
- is the forward pressure still intact at the end
- does this block add something the previous one did not

Do this after the block exists. Checklist thinking during the writing produces careful,
dead prose.

## When the target is a high literary score

If the goal is a calibrated literary score above 8.5, each block additionally needs at
least one of:

- an image concrete enough to still be in the reader's head tomorrow
- a choice or a failure that changes how the reader reads that character
- pressure between what the scene says and what the scene is actually about
- a sentence-level risk that only this book's voice could take
- moral, emotional, or intellectual residue left unresolved

Polished generality is the enemy here. A chapter may be rough, abrupt, or lopsided if that
is what makes it alive.

## Checkpoints

After the opening block, around 25 percent, around 50 percent, and before the final
movement. At each: maintenance check-in, then maintenance check-out. After each chapter:
run the prose gate against the file.

## Output

Chapters into `manuscript/chapters/`. State updated in `PROJECT_STATE.yaml`.
