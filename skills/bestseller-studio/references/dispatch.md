# Bestseller Studio dispatch

This layer turns the pipeline into a specialist team. It still runs on files: each
specialist receives a packet, writes durable outputs, and clears explicit gates.

## The target

The craft target is a calibrated standard for the manuscript and the package. It is not a
promise that any book will become a bestseller or go viral. It is a production threshold:

- no final score before the devil's audit;
- no average hiding a weak floor;
- no market-ready claim without the product, manuscript, package, and launch gates;
- no viral claim without a hypothesis, a framing risk, and a test plan.

## Team order

1. Market researcher
2. Character architect
3. Worldbuilder, where the setting, genre, history, or concept needs rules
4. Theme engineer
5. Plot architect
6. Pacing engineer
7. Prose writer
8. Continuity editor
9. Swarm director
10. Devil's auditor
11. Revision editor
12. Scorekeeper
13. Package strategist
14. Viral framing strategist
15. Launch strategist

A failed gate loops back to the smallest specialist responsible. Do not rewrite the whole
book when the ticket has a local owner.

## Packets

Each packet is written into the project's work folder under the specialist's name, and the
specialist runs from that packet: what to read, what to produce, and the gates its output
must clear.
