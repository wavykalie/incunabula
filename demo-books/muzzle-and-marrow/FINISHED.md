## What this book found, and why the two failures stand

**U1 FAIL — chapter-length CV 12.1%** against a published fiction band of 33.4–45.1%.
**L1 FAIL — 5,314 words short** of the 22,000 floor (declared after the first gate run,
which is itself recorded as a weakness of the contract).

Neither is repaired, and the reason is the same for both: **this pilot's result is the
evidence.** Every earlier book was re-chaptered after the fact; none of them can show what
drafting under the framework *produces*. This book can. Its chapters, drafted with no
per-chapter targets of any kind, landed at CV 12.1% — and the honest position recorded in
`DRAFTING_FRAMEWORK.md` is that the project has never shown that a book drafted without
length targets lands in the band. Editing the chapter lengths now would move the very
measurement the book exists to take. A before-state is evidence, and this is the second
time this project has had to protect one.

A recorded FAIL is worth more than a manufactured pass.

## What was repaired

The **antithesis tic** — U6 FAIL (signature frame in 5/10 chapters) — was a prose defect
and is fixed: four narrative "X is not Y. It is Z" formulas broken, one narrative
"which is why" clause rewritten. Two *spoken* formulas stand (ch 07's "door to/of the
centre", ch 08's "the party on the route"): a character's speech is not the machine's
tic, and rewriting it to satisfy a detector is a privacy measure. After the fix, U6 reads
"stall" 4/10 — the narrator's own frame, at the warn line.
