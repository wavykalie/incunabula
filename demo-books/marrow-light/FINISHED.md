## The length failure, carried as fact

**The book is 21,931 words short of the floor it declared for itself.** 78,069 against a
declared 100,000–112,000. The floor was declared in advance, at premise stage, before a
word of prose existed, and it was missed by 22%.

This colophon does not move the floor, does not explain the shortfall away, and does not
apologise for the book's size. It records the position exactly as the state file framed it
on 2026-09-29 and as the author delegated it on 2026-09-30:

- **The floor was not moved.** A declared target revised after the result is visible is not
  a contract, and that applies to a target that was missed exactly as it applies to one
  that was hit.
- **The book was not padded.** `artifacts/05-outline.md` records that all forty-five
  planned rows are written; nothing architecturally is missing. The book is lean because
  it is lean. Expanding short chapters to reach a number would be a quota chapter written
  after the fact: a short book with a lie in it about why it is short.
- **The delegation is on the record.** The state file reserved this decision for the
  author. On 2026-09-30 the author delegated contract failures to the agent ("go through
  them yourself, fix them"). The agent resolved the breach by the author's own third
  option: carry the failure in the colophon as a fact about the first book drafted under a
  declared-in-advance floor.

A reader of this book gets 78,069 words of it. A reader of this colophon gets the truth
about the number, which is the division of labour this project was built on.
