# COMMONPLACE — the Octavo line

This line's taste ledger, and only this line's. `press.yaml` declares it; nothing
outside this directory is evidence for a Octavo decision, and nothing in it is
enforced against a book outside the line.

The entry types and the ceremony are the same as the root `COMMONPLACE.md`'s —
`decision`, `preference`, `outcome`, `promotion`, with the same formats — and there is
one addition:

**A preference that turns out to hold for another line is not copied across.** It is
merged deliberately, dated, in `press/PROMOTIONS.md`, and the merge names the evidence
that justified it in the second line. A preference that appears in two ledgers by
accident has stopped being either line's, and `python tools/press.py cross` reports it.

This line's register, as declared: short prescriptive nonfiction — the usable thing at the chapter's edge.

**No decisions recorded yet.** The line is declared and the ledger is empty, which is
the honest state of a new line: seeding it with invented taste would make taste a
default, and the root `COMMONPLACE.md` already records what inventing a preference
costs.
