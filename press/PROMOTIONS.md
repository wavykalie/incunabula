# PROMOTIONS — the only channel between the lines

A line reads only its own ledger (`press.yaml`). When a preference earned in one line
turns out to hold in another, it does not get copied: it is merged here, dated, with
the evidence named — and until that exists, the second line does not hold it.

This is the same rule the root ledger keeps between channels (`SELF_IMPROVEMENT.md` §2):
evidence from one surface may not move another. A merge recorded here is the only
sanctioned crossing, and it is the author's to write.

No merges have been made.
