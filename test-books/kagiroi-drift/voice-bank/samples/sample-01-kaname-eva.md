# Sample 01: Kaname EVA Descent (Voice Under Pressure)

The lock cycle took four minutes. Kaname stood with her magnetic boots locked to the ribbed grating, her forehead pressed against the cold acrylic inner faceplate. Her breath smelled of stale tea and copper. Inside the bio-gel suit, her skin was slick with sweat that had cooled to the temperature of river mud.

"Depressurization complete," the chime said. A flat, recorded voice from thirty years ago.

Kaname reached behind her neck, found the primary latch, and twisted. The seal parted with a wet, sucking gasp. Cold station air hit the exposed nape of her neck like a slap. She dragged the helmet off and threw it into the padded cradle, shaking out hair that clung to her temples in greasy black points. Her shoulders were trembling. She counted the six brass rivets along the door frame twice to stop the tremor, but the fourth rivet had a scratch across the head and she couldn't remember if she had made it with her pry-bar or if it had always been there.
