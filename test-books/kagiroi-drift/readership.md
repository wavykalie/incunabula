# Readership Personas: Kagiroi Drift

## 1. PRIMARY PERSONA (Drives Writing Decisions)
- **Name**: Rin (The Yuri Space-Opera Devotee)
- **Profile**: 26, reads adult speculative fiction, yuri manga/light novels, and visceral romantic sci-fi. Loves physical tension, competence-in-crisis, tactile prose, and slow-burning yet explicit romantic and sexual intimacy.
- **Reading Psychology**: Craves intense emotional-somatic immersion. Wants to feel the physical weight, cold alloy, sweat, racing pulse, and breath between women under existential pressure.
- **Deal-Breakers**: Fade-to-black when passion peaks; sanitized or juvenile dialogue; characters who don't feel like real adults with physical desires; generic space stations that feel like suburban living rooms.
- **Emotional Triggers**: Mutual vulnerability after surviving physical danger; the relief of touch after isolation; raw devotion without manufactured melodramatic misunderstandings.
- **Discovery Channels**: Sapphic Book Club recommendations, Yuri sci-fi Reddit communities, Goodreads adult LGBTQ+ sci-fi lists.

## 2. HOSTILE PERSONA (Drives Evaluator Simulation)
- **Name**: Greg (The Hard-SF Purist & Romance Skeptic)
- **Profile**: 44, reads classic hard science fiction (Reynolds, Egan, Baxter). Skeptical of romance-forward plots; hyper-critical of scientific hand-waving, inconsistent orbital mechanics, or artificial emotional drama.
- **Reading Psychology**: Reads to find flaws in the physics, world logic, and structural plausibility. Demands that the station's decay, tether physics, and acoustic telemetry obey consistent internal laws.
- **Deal-Breakers**: Pseudo-science that defies orbital dynamics; melodrama that halts station operations; romance that feels disconnected from the physical environment.
- **Emotional Triggers**: Respects precise, tactile mechanical competence; begrudgingly admires when physical physics directly enforce and heighten emotional and sexual stakes.

## 3. STRETCH PERSONA (Adjacent Genre Cross-Over)
- **Name**: Elena (The Dark Romance & Cyberpunk Enthusiast)
- **Profile**: 31, reads gritty cyberpunk, romantic grimdark, and sensory dark romance. Drawn to neural interfaces, scarred survivors, intense sensory overload, and explicit, raw sexual intimacy.
- **Reading Psychology**: Looks for psychological depth, bodily friction, trauma responses that feel earned, and intense physical passion that transcends clinical environments.
- **Deal-Breakers**: Soft, bloodless romance; lack of sexual chemistry; predictable cozy happy endings without physical cost.
- **Emotional Triggers**: The breaking of emotional control under overwhelming sensory touch; the beauty of biological connection in a cold mechanical world.
