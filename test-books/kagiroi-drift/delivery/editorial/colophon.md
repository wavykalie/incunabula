## 2. Amazon Description (Conversion Edition)

**Between the howling storm of Geryon-7 and the freezing silence of vacuum, the only heat left is between them.**

Hotarubi Kaname is a kinetic hull-diver who cuts through dying ships for a living. Haunted by forty hours spent freezing alone in a decompression pod, she runs on brute physical momentum, calloused muscle, and a desperate hunger for touch that never lets her rest.

Kurotsuyu Sakuya is the station’s telemetry acoustician. Wired directly into the station’s vibrating metal bones via a corporate spinal shunt, every creak and groaning tether vibrates directly through her nervous system. Without rare, black-market neural coolant, the screaming metal will burn out her spine within months.

When the derelict cargo barge *Akegata-Maru* tumbles into their decaying orbit carrying pressurized canisters of the forbidden coolant, Kaname and Sakuya volunteer for a perilous joint EVA dive before corporate recovery cutters arrive to scuttle the wreck. 

Trapped together in a sweltering zero-g reactor bay with failing oxygen and severed tethers, all emotional walls collapse. Surrounded by floating spheres of condensation and the violet pulse of emergency beacons, their mutual desperation erupts into fierce, uninhibited sexual intimacy—Kaname’s heavy, grounding weight silencing Sakuya’s sensory agony, and Sakuya’s worshipful touch thawing the ice in Kaname’s soul.

*Kagiroi Drift* is a searingly sensual, hard-edged adult yuri science fiction novella combining tactile zero-g space salvage, acoustic orbital physics, and explicit, breathless lesbian intimacy.

## 3. Synopsis
In the decaying orbit of gas-giant Geryon-7, atmospheric siphon station *Shin-Murakumo* skims methane while struggling against tether failure. Kaname, an exterior salvor traumatized by past decompression isolation, and Sakuya, an acoustician whose neuro-port translates station vibrations into somatic pain, form an intense somatic bond. Kaname’s heavy physical presence provides the only grounding pressure that calms Sakuya’s neural seizures, leading to a passionate, explicit sexual encounter in Sensor Well 04.

When rogue courier Yoiduki Mizore arrives with news that the derelict *Akegata-Maru* is drifting nearby with military-grade neural-coolant, Kaname and Sakuya launch a high-risk salvage run. Inside the derelict, a micro-meteoroid strike severs their tether, trapping them in the sweltering zero-g reactor bay. Facing imminent re-entry, they shed their suits and surrender to a feverish, raw, weightless sexual encounter.

With the coolant transferred, Sakuya’s mind achieves unprecedented computational clarity. She guides Kaname on an exterior manual thruster burn, enduring brutal 8g deceleration to slingshot the station into permanent Lagrangian orbit while corporate gun-cutters retreat from the thermonuclear exhaust plume. The novella concludes peacefully in the station's observation cupola, where Kaname and Sakuya make tender morning love beneath the golden Kagiroi plasma sunrise of Geryon-7, finally finding sanctuary in the quiet.
