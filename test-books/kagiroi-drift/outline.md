# Chapter Outline: Kagiroi Drift

**Target Length**: Novella (16,000 - 22,000 words, planned 6 chapters, ~2,800 - 3,600 words each).

---

### Chapter 1: The Cold Weld (冷間圧接)
- **POV**: Hotarubi Kaname
- **Structural Approach**: Chronological / In Medias Res (sensory descent from vacuum to interior)
- **Word Target**: ~3,000 words
- **Summary**: Kaname finishes a punishing 14-hour orbital repair on the exterior siphon ring of *Shin-Murakumo*. In Airlock Ring Theta, she struggles out of her frozen, sweat-stuck bio-gel undersuit. Sakuya arrives to log the hull-stress telemetry. The physical contrast between Kaname's overheated, bruised, trembling body and Sakuya's cool, clinical detachment explodes into sharp tactile friction.
- **Emotional Anchor**: The peeling sound of the bio-gel suit releasing suction from Kaname's hip, leaving a red, stinging welt that Sakuya touches with bare, icy fingertips.
- **Emotional Surprise**: Instead of feeling relief after surviving the lethal vacuum, Kaname feels an intense panic in the silent airlock until Sakuya grips her wrist and anchors her.
- **Chaos Moments**: Kaname obsessively clicks her suit's manual purge toggle; Sakuya winces at the high-frequency click, her spinal shunt flashing phantom sparks across her shoulders.
- **Ugly Sentence**: *The suit stank of boiled cabbage and old sweat dried against cold neoprene.*

### Chapter 2: Acoustic Shunt (共鳴孔)
- **POV**: Kurotsuyu Sakuya
- **Structural Approach**: Spiral (deepening interior sensory layers alternating between telemetry readouts and bodily awareness)
- **Word Target**: ~3,200 words
- **Summary**: Inside Sensor Well 04, Sakuya suffers a brutal acoustic spike as tidal gravitational waves from Geryon-7 shake the station's core tethers. Her spinal shunt overheats, threatening neuro-vascular seizure. Kaname breaks protocol and enters the dark, vibrating dome. To ground Sakuya's seizing nervous system, Kaname strips them both and pulls Sakuya beneath her on the thermal pallet. Their desperate physical contact turns into intense, explicit sexual intimacy—Kaname's heavy body, hot mouth, and firm hands completely drowning out the station's deafening acoustic agony.
- **Emotional Anchor**: Sakuya's tears hot against Kaname's clavicle as Kaname enters her with slow, steady fingers, matching the station's deep harmonic pulse until Sakuya's trembling breaks into a breathless, body-shaking orgasm.
- **Emotional Surprise**: Sakuya, who fears sensory contact, begs Kaname not to soften her touch, demanding harder pressure and deeper weight.
- **Chaos Moments**: Sakuya reciting the orbital velocity of Geryon-7 to keep from screaming; Kaname biting Sakuya's collarbone to give her a single, sharp pain to hold onto.
- **Ugly Sentence**: *Her spine felt like someone had driven a heated copper rod through the third cervical vertebra and twisted it twice.*

### Chapter 3: The Ghost Skiff (幽霊艇)
- **POV**: Hotarubi Kaname
- **Structural Approach**: Parallel (two threads: station operations/smuggling negotiations vs. the growing intimacy and possessiveness between Kaname and Sakuya)
- **Word Target**: ~3,000 words
- **Summary**: Shinonome Kagari calls a station conference: a derelict corporate courier skiff, the *Akegata-Maru*, has slipped past perimeter patrols into a decaying orbital trajectory. Rogue courier Yoiduki Mizore has docked in a damaged stealth skiff, bringing word that the *Akegata-Maru* carries pressurized canisters of military-grade neural-coolant—the exact compound Sakuya needs to survive. But corporate recovery cutters are already matching orbits. Kaname volunteers to lead a high-risk kinetic EVA salvage run. Sakuya forbids her, terrified of losing her; their argument leads to a fierce, desperate confrontation in the narrow maintenance conduit.
- **Emotional Anchor**: Kaname pinning Sakuya against the conduit wall, tasting the lingering salt on Sakuya's lips while Sakuya's hands knot desperately into Kaname's undershirt.
- **Emotional Surprise**: Kagari, normally cynical and detached, quietly leaves a ration of contraband sugar and a magnetic seal patch outside Kaname's bunk without a word.
- **Chaos Moments**: Yoiduki tossing a silver coolant seal from hand to hand; Kaname's knee giving a sharp, phantom twinge from cold-nitrogen exposure.
- **Ugly Sentence**: *The station grease tasted like mutton fat mixed with lithium soap.*

### Chapter 4: Thermal Breach (熱破断)
- **POV**: Alternating (Kaname and Sakuya)
- **Structural Approach**: Fragmented / Cut (rapid cuts between Sakuya's telemetry station and Kaname's breach inside the derelict)
- **Word Target**: ~3,500 words
- **Summary**: Kaname and Sakuya both deploy to the *Akegata-Maru*—Kaname to cut the cargo seals, Sakuya to stabilize the derelict's failing telemetry. Inside the dark, freezing ghost ship, a structural bulkhead collapses under micro-meteoroid stress, severing their primary tether. Trapped in a pressurized core compartment with failing environmental controls, surrounded by escaping coolant steam and zero-g condensation, the imminent threat of suffocation dissolves all remaining emotional distance. In the sweltering, damp microgravity of the reactor bay, they share their suits' oxygen and surrender to a feverish, raw, deeply sensual sexual encounter, bodies clinging and moving together in weightlessness.
- **Emotional Anchor**: Spheres of sweat and lubricant floating in the low-g airlock, reflecting the violet pulse of the emergency beacon as Kaname takes Sakuya with her mouth against the warm reactor housing.
- **Emotional Surprise**: Sakuya laughing softly amidst the terrifying steam, experiencing complete peace while stranded on a dying ship.
- **Chaos Moments**: Kaname's suit HUD blinking an annoying low-battery warning while she is kissing Sakuya's bare breasts; Sakuya tracing a jagged scar on Kaname's hip with complete reverence.
- **Ugly Sentence**: *The coolant steam smelled sharply of rotting fish and ammonia.*

### Chapter 5: Resonance Cascade (連鎖崩壊)
- **POV**: Kurotsuyu Sakuya
- **Structural Approach**: Chronological / Acceleration (rapid pacing as the orbital crisis peaks)
- **Word Target**: ~3,300 words
- **Summary**: Armed corporate recovery cutters arrive at the periphery of the *Shin-Murakumo*. The station's counterweight tether snaps under the tidal pull of Geryon-7, sending the ring into an uncontrolled atmospheric dive. With the new neural-coolant circulating in her shunt, Sakuya's mind operates at unprecedented clarity. She becomes the station's sensory nexus, calculating the exact manual burn trajectory. Kaname undertakes a suicidal EVA run to manually ignite the station's auxiliary hydrogen boosters. Guided by Sakuya's voice and acoustic telemetry in her helmet, Kaname executes the burn, nearly burning her limbs through her suit as the station shudders through the upper cloud deck.
- **Emotional Anchor**: Sakuya's steady, rhythmic breathing in Kaname's headset—the only sound that keeps Kaname conscious as gravitational g-forces crush her ribs.
- **Emotional Surprise**: The corporate cutters do not attack; watching the miraculous manual burn, they break orbit and retreat rather than risk collision with the plummeting debris.
- **Chaos Moments**: Sakuya's nose bleeding onto the acoustic console while she recalculates the burn vector; Kaname cursing violently at a jammed manual toggle with fierce, triumphant joy.
- **Ugly Sentence**: *Her ribs cracked like dry pine kindling under eight gravities of brutal deceleration.*

### Chapter 6: The Kagiroi Dawn (陽炎の朝)
- **POV**: Hotarubi Kaname
- **Structural Approach**: Epistolary / Deceleration (contemplative, sensory-rich resolution)
- **Word Target**: ~3,200 words
- **Summary**: The *Shin-Murakumo* has stabilized in a safe, permanent resonant orbit. The coolant has halted Sakuya's neural degeneration. In the station's quietest observation cupola, the giant gas-planet Geryon-7 rises below them in a staggering, radiant dawn of amber, gold, and violet—the true *Kagiroi Effect* of glowing atmospheric plasma. Kaname and Sakuya lie together on a thick mattress under the stars, naked, bruised, their skin glowing in the sunrise. They make slow, languid, tender love in the morning light, their bodies completely attuned, no longer fighting the void but having carved out an unshakable haven within it.
- **Emotional Anchor**: Kaname pressing her lips to the quiet, cooled spinal port at the nape of Sakuya's neck, tasting sweet skin and morning warmth.
- **Emotional Surprise**: Kaname realizes she is not afraid of the silence anymore; the quiet space between them feels like home.
- **Chaos Moments**: A half-eaten tube of sweetened synthetic paste drifting lazily past the edge of the mattress; Sakuya idly tracing Kaname's pulse with a small mechanical caliper.
- **Ugly Sentence**: *She had three missing toenails and her left shoulder was black and purple, but her mouth on Sakuya's throat was softer than silk.*
