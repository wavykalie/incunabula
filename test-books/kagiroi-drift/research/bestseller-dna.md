# Bestseller DNA: Kagiroi Drift

## Section 1: Core Machine
- **Sensory Reality**: Space is not cold abstraction; it is physical punishment. Suits smell of dried sweat and recycled ozone; bulkheads groan with tidal shear; metal conducted vibration is felt in the teeth before heard in the ear.
- **Erotic Gravity**: Intimacy is not polite choreography. It is visceral relief from the crushing hostility of vacuum. Touch is weight, heat, grounding, and salvation.
- **Thematic Resonance**: Every proper noun and piece of technology embodies the philosophical tension between ephemeral thermal flare (*kagiroi*) and freezing, permanent void (*kurotsuyu*).

## Section 2: Prose Rules for the Writer
1. **Zero Overprint**: State comparisons raw and stark. Never explain the metaphor. Never follow a simile with an interpretive restatement.
2. **Tactile Primacy**: Anchor every scene in concrete physical friction—skin temperature, calloused pads of fingers, the seal of silicone neck-dams, the drag of magnetic soles on rusted plating.
3. **Dialogue Under Pressure**: People in orbital survival do not speak in balanced thesis statements. They cut words short, interrupt, drop syllables, and let silence carry the weight.
4. **No Slop Constructions**: Strictly eliminate AI tells: no 'testament to', 'tapestry of', 'dance of', 'shivers down spines', 'little did she know', 'it was as if', 'a reminder that'.
5. **Ugly Sentence Mandate**: Every chapter must contain at least one rough, blunt, unadorned sentence that sounds unmistakably human and unpolished.
