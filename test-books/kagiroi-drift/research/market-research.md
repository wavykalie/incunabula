# Market Research: Kagiroi Drift

## Field
The adult science fiction romance and yuri space opera market combines high-concept orbital worldbuilding with visceral, emotionally charged sapphic relationships. Readers in this niche demand hard-edged, atmospheric physical stakes rather than sanitized space-fantasy tropes. The intersection of explicit sensual intimacy with visceral industrial science fiction (tether mechanics, zero-g physiology, sensory cybernetics) fills a proven gap between soft cozy sci-fi and emotionally distant hard SF.

## Patterns
### Shared Conventions
- High-stakes physical environments where failure means decompression, asphyxiation, or kinetic catastrophe.
- Intimacy as an anchor against vast, indifferent cosmic scale.
- Non-standard, deeply atmospheric naming and technical vocabulary.

### Absent (The Opening)
- Most queer space opera defaults either to witty banter or grand galactic empires. Almost none explore the grim, tactile, industrial reality of deep-space salvage divers and acoustic telemetry operators living in freezing, decaying orbital siphon stations where physical touch is the only defense against neurological breakdown.
- Explicit, visceral sexual intimacy that honors real body heat, sweat in zero-g, unhurried tactile relief, and muscular vulnerability.

### Reader Complaints in Comp Titles
- Too cerebral/distant; emotional intimacy kept off-page or fade-to-black.
- Space stations feeling like suburban hotels rather than dangerous, metallic, leaking pressure vessels.
- Generic character naming that clashes with speculative worldbuilding.

## Positioning
- Gap: Tactile, industrial hard-scifi yuri novella with unapologetic, explicit, sensual adult intimacy, zero-g kinetic salvage, and non-standard thematic anime-style nomenclature.
- Target Audience: Adult sapphic fiction readers, yuri enthusiasts, hard sci-fi readers who appreciate sensual friction and tactile prose.
- Length Target: Novella / short novel of 16,000 to 22,000 words across 6 focused, muscular chapters.
- Primary Engagement: Empathy + Fascination (sensory immersion in orbital physics and somatic intimacy).
