# Premise: Kagiroi Drift

## premise_axes
- profession: EVA kinetic salvor and deep-hull orbital cutter
- engine: decaying station tether resonance salvage and black-market coolant containment
- relationship: touch-starved acoustic hull-diver and sensor-overloaded astrogator

## Narrative Engine
In the decaying orbit of gas-giant Geryon-7, the industrial siphon-station *Shin-Murakumo* is slowly losing its orbital tether tension. Hotarubi Kaname, a reckless, touch-starved kinetic salvor haunted by two days spent freezing in a decompression pod, spends her waking hours cutting through collapsing derelict hulls to keep the station from burning up in the atmosphere. 

Kurotsuyu Sakuya, the station's reclusive telemetry acoustician, is wired directly into the hull via a neuro-acoustic spinal shunt. The structural agony of the dying station vibrates directly through Sakuya's nervous system, causing sensory overload that only deliberate, overwhelming tactile pressure and heavy, grounding body heat can alleviate. 

When a ghost freighter carrying illicit neuro-coolant drifts into their decaying sector, Kaname and Sakuya must make a perilous zero-g salvage dive before corporate recovery cutters seize it. In the cramped, steaming compartments between freezing vacuum and burning thruster flares, their intense physical and romantic connection erupts into raw, passionate sexual intimacy—grounding Sakuya's shattered nerves and thawing Kaname's frozen heart as they fight to save their station and claim a life together.
