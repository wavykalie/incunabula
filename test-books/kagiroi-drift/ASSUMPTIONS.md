# Assumptions & Inferences: Kagiroi Drift

This document records working inferences that have not yet been superseded by explicit author direction.

## Inferred Working Hypotheses
1. **Positioning & Length**: Positioned as a novella / short novel of 16,000 to 22,000 words across 6 chapters.
2. **Explicit Yuri / Intimacy Level**: Adult, highly sensual and explicit sexual content integrated into character psychology and environmental tension, rather than fade-to-black or decorative erotica.
3. **World Mechanics**: Harder-edged space salvage aesthetic; radio silence from magnetosphere justifies acoustic structural telemetry; tether physics drives the urgent crisis.
4. **Nomenclature System**: Anime-inspired Japanese compound names with direct poetic and mechanical meaning (*Hotarubi* = firefly light, *Kurotsuyu* = black dew, *Shinonome* = dawn fissure, *Yoiduki* = dusk moon).
