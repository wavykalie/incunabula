# Voice Matrix: Kagiroi Drift

## 1. Global Narrative Voice Profile
- **Stance**: Tactile, intimate, unsparing, somatically grounded third-person close.
- **Rhythm**: Rapid, muscular acceleration during EVA and physical crisis; deep, sensory deceleration during intimate, domestic, and sexual scenes.
- **Sensory Priority**: Pressure, friction, temperature, acoustic resonance, smell of ozone and sweat, taste of salt and copper. Avoid sterile visual-only narration.
- **Rules**:
  - Never explain what a simile means.
  - Zero overprints ("X is not Y. It is Z").
  - Em-dash density strictly under 7 per 1,000 words.
  - No AI cliches or filler adverbs.

## 2. Character Voice Cards

### Hotarubi Kaname (Lead EVA Salvor)
- **Speech Patterns**: Blunt, vernacular, sentence fragments, physical imperatives. Drops pronouns when stressed ("Need a torque wrench", "Got it").
- **Syntax Fingerprint**: Short, punchy, active verbs; tactile nouns (alloy, grease, grip, burn).
- **Metaphor Source**: Industrial metalwork, kinetic velocity, thermal thresholds, combustion.
- **Voice Under Pressure**:
  - *At Rest*: Low, dry, teasing, slightly husky, voice carrying the gravel of dry atmosphere.
  - *In Crisis*: Compressed, clipped, barking essential commands, cursing flatly without melodrama.
  - *In Intimacy*: Vulnerable, rough-edged, breathless, repeating Sakuya's name like a prayer; murmuring raw, unvarnished physical desire.
- **Benchmark Line**: *"Stop looking at the telemetry and let me pull you down. Your skin is freezing, Sakuya."*

### Kurotsuyu Sakuya (Telemetry Acoustician)
- **Speech Patterns**: Precise, cadence measured, technically exact, but emotionally resonant. Uses soft consonants and deliberate pauses.
- **Syntax Fingerprint**: Balanced clauses, acoustic terminology (harmonics, shear, frequency, dampening), sensory adjectives.
- **Metaphor Source**: Fluid dynamics, sound waves, planetary resonance, shadow, condensation.
- **Voice Under Pressure**:
  - *At Rest*: Measured, calm, formal, quiet.
  - *In Crisis*: Sentences fragment into numbers and frequencies; voice thins to a breathless whisper as neural overload strikes.
  - *In Intimacy*: Disarmed, gasping, helpless yet demanding, abandoning technical composure to plead for weight and friction.
- **Benchmark Line**: *"The hull isn't quiet today. It's vibrating at sixty hertz... right where your hands are pressing."*

### Shinonome Kagari (Watch-Officer)
- **Speech Patterns**: Weary, sarcastic, dry, colloquial station pidgin.
- **Syntax Fingerprint**: Compound sentences linked with 'and', trailing ellipses, cynical rhetorical questions.
- **Metaphor Source**: Bureaucracy, fuel ratios, docking fees, cigarette smoke.
- **Benchmark Line**: *"If you two burn up out there, I'm logging it as scheduled maintenance so we don't pay the salvage tax."*

### Yoiduki Mizore (Rogue Courier)
- **Speech Patterns**: Quick, guarded, whisper-soft, streetwise.
- **Syntax Fingerprint**: Elliptical, ambiguous, rapid-fire questions, evasive answers.
- **Metaphor Source**: Cold stealth, orbital shadows, black markets.
- **Benchmark Line**: *"The seal is military grade. You touch it wrong, it vents twenty liters of nitrogen in your face."*

## 4. Anti-Pattern Checklist

- [ ] No "the truth was simple"
- [ ] No "danced across her skin"
- [ ] No "shivers ran down her spine"
