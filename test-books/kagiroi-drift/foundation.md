# Foundation: Kagiroi Drift

## 1. Theme (Formulated as Question)
**Can two traumatized women surviving on the edge of a dying orbital station claim bodily intimacy and mutual devotion without burning each other out like short-lived flares?**

## 2. Engagement Ranking
- **Primary**: Empathy (intense resonance with Kaname's touch-starved isolation and Sakuya's sensory agony).
- **Secondary**: Fascination (tactile industrial space salvage, acoustic orbital telemetry, tether dynamics).
- **Tertiary**: Self-Insertion / Aspiration (the visceral wish for deep, grounding, unconditional physical and emotional sanctuary).

## 3. Stylistic Device & Voice
- **Stylistic Device**: *The Somatic Acoustic Sensorium* — the sensory boundary between external vacuum and internal body heat.
- **Narrative Voice**: Third-person close alternating between Hotarubi Kaname and Kurotsuyu Sakuya.
- **Tone**: Visceral, industrial, erotic, tactile, atmospheric, muscular.

## 4. Cultural & Technical Vocabulary
- **Kagiroi Effect** (陽炎効果): The optical and acoustic shimmer where extreme thermal differential meets relativistic vacuum; also refers to the phantom heat of touch remembered in isolation.
- **Siphon-Ring** (吸管環): The rotating atmospheric skimmer skimming the upper cloud deck of Geryon-7.
- **Kinetic Salvor / Hull-Diver** (船体潜手): EVA salvors who use cold-welding cutters and magnetic tethers in vacuum.
- **Neuro-Acoustic Shunt** (神経音響シャント): Spinal implants that translate station hull-resonance directly into somatic neural input.
- **Gel-Liner** (生体緩衝内衣): The pressurized silicone undersuit that sticks to bare skin with sweat and bio-electric charge.

## 5. Detailed Character Profiles with CHAOS

### Hotarubi Kaname (蛍火 要) — Lead Hull-Diver / Kinetic Salvor
- **Thematic Meaning**: *Hotarubi* (firefly light in the dark) + *Kaname* (keystone/pivot).
- **Physique & Aesthetic**: Lithe, athletic, feminine beauty with sculpted washboard abs and narrow waist (Etie-like aesthetic); short cropped raven hair, delicate features with intense dark eyes; graceful kinetic salvage stamina rather than bulky mass.
- **Core Wound**: Survived forty hours freezing in an emergency decompression pod of the *Minato-9*, listening to her crewmates' breathing cease one by one in total silence.
- **The Lie She Believes**: "If I stop moving, stop working, or let anyone see how cold I am inside, I will freeze to death."
- **Arc**: Learns that surviving does not require reckless self-destruction; surrenders her fear of stillness to Sakuya's steady touch.
- **Chaos Dimensions**:
  - *Irrelevant Obsession*: Habitually counts the thread-turns on hydraulic bleeder valves with her thumbnail until the cuticle bleeds.
  - *Cognitive Distortion*: Conflates quiet warmth with impending decompression failure.
  - *Unprompted Memory*: The specific metallic smell of frozen coffee crystals floating inside a ruptured flight deck.
  - *Failed Emotional Management*: Bites her own lower lip until it tastes of copper whenever an EVA tether goes slack, trying to trigger pain to suppress vertigo.

### Kurotsuyu Sakuya (玄露 朔夜) — Station Telemetry Acoustician
- **Thematic Meaning**: *Kurotsuyu* (black dew / dark condensation) + *Sakuya* (new moon night / deep shadow).
- **Core Wound**: Spinal neuro-acoustic integration performed by an exploitative corporate mining consortium, leaving her nervous system permanently fused to structural hull frequencies without adequate coolant maintenance.
- **The Lie She Believes**: "I am only an instrument for the station's agony; my body cannot handle human contact without burning out."
- **Arc**: Discovers that Kaname's heavy, firm, unhurried tactile weight does not overload her implants—it provides the physical shield that quiets the noise.
- **Chaos Dimensions**:
  - *Irrelevant Obsession*: Memorizing the serial numbers of abandoned telemetry beacons.
  - *Cognitive Distortion*: Believes that if she does not consciously brace for every micro-fracture in the station's hull, the station will instantly shear in half.
  - *Unprompted Memory*: The cold porcelain tile floor of the orbital clinic where her spinal shunt was first calibrated.
  - *Failed Emotional Management*: Trembles uncontrollably when someone whispers directly into her ear, mistaking the air current for a vacuum leak.

### Shinonome Kagari (東雲 篝) — Station Watch-Officer & Salvage Broker
- **Thematic Meaning**: *Shinonome* (dawn fissure) + *Kagari* (watchfire beacon).
- **Function**: Pragmatic anchor who oversees the docking umbilical; acts as a cynical buffer against encroaching debt collectors.

### Yoiduki Mizore (宵月 霙) — Rogue Biotechnical Courier
- **Thematic Meaning**: *Yoiduki* (evening moon) + *Mizore* (freezing sleet).
- **Function**: Smuggles the experimental neural-coolant aboard the crippled *Akegata-Maru*.

## 6. Opening Strategy (Chapter 1)
- **NOT** a competence cascade or an info-dump on the world.
- Begins with the claustrophobic, physical reality of unpeeling a pressurized vacuum suit after an 18-hour dive: the tearing of dried sweat from ribs, the sound of blood in the ears, the smell of ozone, and the overwhelming sensory shock of cold skin meeting warm skin in the decompressing airlock.

## 7. Re-Read Architecture
- Chapter 1 plants the subtle harmonic shudder in Sensor Well 04 that Sakuya conceals; on re-read, the reader recognizes this shudder as the dying tether sequence that dictates the final climax in Chapter 5.
- Kaname's habit of checking Sakuya's spinal junction port carries an intimate tactile ritual that shifts from desperate maintenance to tender sexual devotion.

## 8. Anti-AI / Writer Warnings
- **No Overprint Similes**: Write raw sensory facts ("The hull was frozen iron", not "The hull was like a frozen iron beast waiting to awaken").
- **No Sanitized Intimacy**: Depict real female bodies: sweat, friction, flushed skin, musk, ragged breathing, vaginal wetness, muscular exhaustion, and the profound relief of oral and manual intimacy.
- **No Cliché Filler Phrases**: Avoid all generic AI tropes.
