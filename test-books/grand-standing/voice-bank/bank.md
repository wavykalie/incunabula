# VOICE BANK — Grand Standing (phase 2.5)

Ten samples: 3 under pressure (**P**), 2 irrelevant thoughts (**I**), 5 at
rest. Blind-sortable against `voice-matrix.md` benchmarks.

## S1 — V1 Marnie, rest
"The council chamber smells of warm dust and radiator paint. Nine members sat.
The public gallery held four people, two of whom were, by profession, the
press. I am including myself in that count to be accurate."

## S2 — V1 Marnie, pressure (P)
"He falsified the measurement. I had the original in my bag. I said: the
survey says stage. He said: she's safe. I wrote nothing. Filed nothing. For
three days I wrote nothing at all."

## S3 — V1 Marnie, irrelevant thought (I)
"The chamber's lettering is 1969 Gill Sans, cut deep, gold leaf gone to
mustard. You can date a civic building by its apostrophes. The theatre's
apostrophes are newer than its commas — the signage was replaced twice. Nobody
replaces signage on a building they expect to keep."

## S4 — V2 Desmond, rest
"A small paper survives the way a river stone survives, Miss Liss: by not
being the rock that argues with the current. Coffee? The machine is dying and
we shall outlive it by a fortnight, both of us being cheaper to run."

## S5 — V2 Desmond, pressure (P)
"Run it. The survey, run it on the front. All of it — including the page-three
ad facing the story I killed in ninety-eight, so the readers can see exactly
what I charged myself. I will not have my obituary written by a man who never
met a deadline."

## S6 — V2 Desmond, irrelevant thought (I)
"The possessive of a singular noun ending in s takes no extra s in this office
and never has. James' car. The style guide is from 1961 and I will be
interred with it."

## S7 — V3 Ruth, rest
"Let's be clear: the working group is not a delaying tactic. It is a
deliberate instrument of process. The lilies are wrong again — lilies are for
remembrance, we are not remembering anything yet. Agenda item four."

## S8 — V3 Ruth, pressure (P)
"There was never three options. There was one budget line and I have known
this since the fourteenth. Do not write that down. ...Write it down. The pot
says the lid is black — my mother says this — and I am the pot."

## S9 — V4 Arkwright, rest
"Counterweights don't lie. The grid's honest: four hundred tons of iron tells
you what it is. It's people who come in here with their survey forms and their
— she's not a building, that's what I'm saying."

## S10 — V4 Arkwright, pressure (P) + irrelevant thought (I)
"You held them. Six thousand and you held every one. (Row G. 1969 mohair
blend, seventy per cent — you can feel the twist through a glove. Nobody
replaces mohair. She's the only one left who knows what the opening night
smelled like and I'm — I'm talking to a building again.)"
