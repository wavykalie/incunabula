# OUTLINE — Grand Standing (phase 2, forme)

32 chapters × ~3,000 words. Contract [95,000–130,000]; plan 96,000.
Every chapter carries an **emotional anchor** (what it is about, emotionally)
and an **emotional surprise** (the turn the reader does not expect).
Structural approaches rotate; **no approach repeats in consecutive chapters**.

Structural approaches used (R1–R8): R1 dispatch/file · R2 scene-in-scene
(a play or meeting rendered as reportage) · R3 investigation (quest thread) ·
R4 portrait (one person, held) · R5 montage of voices (chorus) · R6 countdown
(dated entries toward the deadline) · R7 digression (the narrator goes off on
fonts, letters, fabric) · R8 document collage (minutes, letters, invoices).

## Transition plan (five join types, none used twice in a row)

J1 time-jump ("three weeks later") · J2 image echo (the closing image recurs
in the next opening) · J3 question-answer (a chapter's last question is the
next chapter's first line) · J4 counterpoint (the next chapter contradicts the
previous one's mood deliberately) · J5 object-carrier (an object — ring, seat,
letter — travels between chapters). Sequence used across the outline:
J3,J1,J5,J2,J4,J1,J3,J5,J2,J4,J3,J1,J5,J2,J4,J1,J3,J5,J2,J4,J3,J1,J5,J2,J4,J1,J3,J5,J2,J4,J1,J5 —
no consecutive repeats.
