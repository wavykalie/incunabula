# Foundation — Stall Twelve

## 1. Characters (wound/lie/want/need + chaos, compact)
**Delia Marsh, 56 — dumpling stall 12 (POV).** Wound: husband Ray died 6 years ago owing the stall's suppliers; she kept the stall, never the grief. Lie: *if the fold is perfect, the debt stays put.* Want: stop the eviction. Need: grieve out loud once. Contradictions: feeds everyone, eats standing; fearless with officials, frightened of forms; counts change twice, never counts ghosts. Voice: short practical, flour nouns; humour dry, worst-moment. Never says "closure/journey" sincerely. Obsession: oil temperature by sound. Distortion: wipes surfaces when anxious. Memory: Ray's thumb on a dumpling seal. Failed management: files eviction letters unopened by date. Arc: positive — files nothing, says it aloud ch18.

**Harold Pye, 71-at-death (1974) — founder-ghost, stall 12.** Wound: died owing the market nothing and the council everything (the lease). Lie: *paper outlasts people, so paper is the people.* Want: find the 1962 indenture. Need: be remembered by the living, not the lease. Contradictions: pompous diction, weepy at fryers; haunts one stall, afraid of the dark storeroom. Voice: municipal Edwardian, collapses into market vernacular when oil is hot. Tic: "Clause—" opener. Rules (stated ch2, never broken): (1) remembers only when fryers are on; (2) can't touch paper, only point; (3) can't leave the market footprint. Obsession: margarine vs butter (1974 schism). Arc: positive — points at the living, not the lease, ch19.

**Mr. Fenn, 48 — council jobsworth.** Wound: grew up over a stall his father lost. Lie: *a clean file hurts less.* Want: signatures by Friday(s). Need: admit some closings are choices. Contradictions: kind to pigeons, ruthless on paper; hates markets, knows every pitch. Voice: conditional smoothness, form numbers. Never raises voice. Arc: flat — exits with unsigned pen, one kindness (pigeons fed).

**Sanna? no — Priya is taken (Freeze 4 collision: Priya exists in the-origin-point + Hollow Bridge). Teen: LEO Marsh, 16 — grandson, camera.** Wants out (film school). Tic: "Is this the—?" no — that's Nico's; Leo: "Say that again" + records. Pressure: quiet-competent. Arc: stays for the siege, films the lease, leaves with footage + bone? no — leaves with the rent-book.

**Mrs. Anand? taken (kitsch). Rival: BIG CONNIE, 60 — jerk-chicken stall, rival-turned-ally.** Voice: volume + proverbs. Mini: **Dr. Shah, health inspector** — form-speech, "for the record" no — that's Bex's; Shah: "Schedule 4," clipboard, secretly feeds Pye's pigeons.

Mirrors: Pye exposes Delia's paper-as-avoidance; Fenn her price; Connie her stubbornness-as-love; Leo the future holder.

## 2. Voice guide
Close third on Delia, present tense for market action, past for Pye-memory. Avg 14-18w; short flat for procedure, accumulative for food. Plain trade speech + flour/oil nouns; Latinate for council papers only. Comparisons from market work (oil, dough, steam), never orchestral/nautical/cosmic. Humour worst-moment dry; farce from logistics, never cruelty. Banned: stacked em-dash parentheticals, *the kind of X that Y*, answered rhetorical Qs, *there was something about*, *and just like that*, therapy naming, explanatory coda closings, antithesis *not X but Y*.

## 3. Pace (comic long novel, 20 ch x ~5,000w = ~100,000w)
Alternate siege/fallout: PRESSURE (paper/deadline), STALL (market-day procedure), TALE (Pye-memory leak), ROOM (two-hander), AFTER (breather). No consecutive repeats. Shortest >=40% below average; longest <=50% above. End on image/gag-withdrawn, never moral.

## 4. Emotional curve (full per-chapter in outline)
Oscillation: warmth <-> threat; no 3 consecutive same-dominant; post-peak (>=8) drops to 4-6; catharsis only after vulnerability; most devastating never last.

## 5. High-impact beats (setup >=2 ch earlier)

Delia says grief aloud (ch18) — setup filed-letters ch1, Ray's thumb ch1, Connie's parallel ch13. 3. ## 6. Theme as question
Macro: what do you owe a place that only exists because people keep showing up? Never stated. Medium: personal (Delia's debt) -> structural (who profits from flats) -> existential (can a market hold a debt?). Micro per chapter. Nano: oil sound, folded seals, rent books, pigeons, fryer steam, unopened letters.

## 7. Opening strategy
In motion on procedure: pre-dawn fold + oil-by-sound, first wrong detail by p3 (cold fryer, warm stall). No preamble; eviction arrives as paperweight (unopened letters) ch1, on stage ch4.

## 8. Re-read architecture
Second read is a paper read: every lease clue visible but misfiled; Pye's leaks re-read as testimony; ch20 buy-out replays ch6 whip-round beat-for-beat.

## 9. Stylistic device
Fryer-leak testimony: each TALE chapter opens mid-memory (Pye mid-sentence, oil hot) that turns into alibi/motive/receipt. Oral vs catalogue: the market remembers aloud; the council files silent.

## 10. Engagement ranking
1. Empathy 2. Fascination 3. Intellectual 4. Aspiration (keeper identity) 5. Self-insertion (low — Delia specific).
