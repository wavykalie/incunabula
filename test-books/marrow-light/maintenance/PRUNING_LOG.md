# PRUNING LOG — MARROW LIGHT

Append-only. **Records restraint as well as removal — a decision not to prune is an
entry.** Per `incunabula-codex/references/maintenance-protocol.md`.

The temptation in a book this size is to start removing things in Block IV because the
chapters are getting complicated. The log exists so that a later session can see every
time a thing was almost deleted, and why it wasn't, and whether the reason still holds.

---

## 2026-09-29 — Not pruning: the two dead women

**Candidates:** OFF-001 Solveig Havelock, OFF-002 Vera Marrow.

Both are structurally necessary and both are the kind of thing a revision pass would
reasonably propose cutting for pace. Recorded here, before the manuscript exists, so that
the decision is made once and does not get re-litigated at chapter 30 when the book is
tired.

**Why Solveig stays.** She is referenced in nine chapters and watched once. A reader who
has read chapter 43 and then encounters a draft without her has been handed a book where
a person died for atmosphere, and the whole design — the tape, the invoices, the back
room, the fact that Fabiola had the number for four years — is aimed at making her
*specific*. Cutting her to make the pace better would be exactly the move the project has
already logged as the recurring failure: **the thing that is doing the emotional work
removed in the name of efficiency.** She is nine minutes long. That is the correct length
for a person and it is not negotiable.

**Why Vera stays.** She is never in a room. She is on a monitor, on a bad connection, and
in a tape. She is four appearances and she is the reason Ottoline is who she is, and the
book's central prohibition — *these two women are each what the other would have to become*
— collapses into a two-hander about equipment if Vera is cut.

**Standing instruction for later sessions:** if a revision pass proposes removing either,
check whether the *other* off-page figure is doing the same work. If they are
interchangeable, one can go. They are not interchangeable: one is dead and present in the
room, one is alive and permanently on a screen. Different instruments, different pressure.

## 2026-09-29 — Not pruning: the four blocks' length variety

**Candidate:** the block structure (9 / 10 / 9 / 9 / 8 chapters) looks irregular and
could be "tidied" to five equal blocks of nine.

**Not tidied.** A book with five identical block lengths is a book whose shape has been
designed to produce a distribution, and the framework exists because the project has
already made that mistake twice and traced it. The block lengths are what the four turns
came out as. If a uniformity gate disagrees later, the gate is recording information about
the book and **that is what it is for** — see `PROJECT_STATE.yaml`, `gates.uniformity`,
and the withdrawn table in `DRAFTING_FRAMEWORK.md`.

**Standing instruction:** do not equalise block lengths to move U1. Record U1.

## 2026-09-29 — Recorded: the two ambiguities in the contract

**Not a pruning action.** Logged because it is the sort of thing a later tidy-up pass would
collapse into one device and thereby destroy.

`THREAD-03` (the ambiguous clause in the ch 2 contract) is **two different ambiguities,
not one recurring one**: a legal one in Block I, about material neither party owns, and a
hydraulic one in Block III, about a gauge reading in 1994. They read as a pattern to a
reader who is enjoying the pattern. Merging them would produce a single clever thing where
the book has a coincidence that the characters are not clever enough to see. **The
coincidence is the point.**
