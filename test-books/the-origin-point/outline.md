## Act I — PROCEDURE (ch 1–6)

**Throughline:** a fire investigator is told what a fire was before she is told what
it was. Every chapter of Act I contains at least one moment where someone tells her
something true that makes the truth less available.

| ch | title | words | structure | hook (opening) | pull (ending) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Three Inside | 3,200 | scene-forward, single POV | Rusk signs the certification before she walks the scene. She is twenty minutes early and it is the last time she will ever be that early. | The origin point is wrong. Not ambiguous-wrong. *Wrong*, in the specific way that means it was poured. |
| 2 | Origin Point | 4,100 | investigation | Rusk has poured a hundred of these in training and has never once been asked to certify one. | The accelerant pooled. It was bought in volume. Two people had access to bulk purchase. One of them is at her mother's house. |
| 3 | What the Neighbors Say | 1,500 | interlude, ensemble scatter | Seven people, four sentences each, and every single one of them is helpful. | Sam Okafor says "we don't really have a fire here." Nobody corrects him. |
| 4 | Six Years | 3,900 | retrospective interior | Rusk has read the 2019 determination four hundred times and has never once read it as arson. | The determination cites a circuit she cannot find in the file. **And the number: one person died in that house in 2019, and Rusk signed the form that said it was a fault in a circuit that was replaced in 2016.** |
| 5 | The Garage | 2,580 | interrogation | Denny Aylward answers every question, in order, without being asked to. | He sold 340 gallons in 2019. He sold 260 in April. **And the 260 went out in a truck he does not own.** |
| 6 | The Room Nobody Sleeps In | 4,600 | investigation | Rusk realizes the three were found in a room with no beds, and that this is not a detail, it is a statement. | The room is the **study** — the one room in that house nobody sleeps in, where a family keeps the things it means to keep. **The 2019 house has an identical study. That is the signature, and she does not say so.** |

## Act IV — CONSEQUENCE (ch 23–30)

**Throughline:** the case is solved and the question is not answered. Per the conceded
`A8`, Rusk's own culpability is resolved on the page. The town's is offered, not
insisted upon. This act is where the book's one craft latitude lives.

| ch | title | words | structure | hook | pull |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 23 | After the Certification | 1,700 | short scene | The form is signed. It takes four minutes. She has thought about it for six years. | She signs her own complicity out loud, in a room, to a person, and the person lets her. |
| 24 | What Terrell Wanted | 4,800 | character interior | The motive, laid out whole, with no forgiveness attached to it. | He was not avenging Cora. He was *finishing* what Cora could not. **And here, on the page, he learns that the 2019 fire killed somebody — which is the whole basis of CONF-004 and was not on the page until this paragraph.** |
| 25 | Denny's Account | 2,100 | confession-adjacent | Denny has been waiting nineteen years to tell someone and has picked the wrong moment, twice. | He sold the fuel. He was told what it was for. He has the name of who told him. |
| 26 | The Long Afternoon | 4,200 | procedural | The file was closed on Friday and reopened by nobody, because a closed file does not reopen — it is simply worked, by whoever is willing. | There is no deadline, and Rusk does not know what to do with a case that is not trying to leave. **This resolves ch 13, twenty chapters later.** |
| 27 | Cora | 2,000 | two-hander | A mother and a daughter, and the only scene in the book with no evidence in it. | Cora: "I didn't light it. I made it true." **The theme, answered by a character, not by the narrator.** |
| 28 | The Ones Who Agreed | 3,100 | ensemble | Six of the seven are still in town. They are not leaving. | The town does not disperse, does not confess, and does not change. It simply continues, having been told. |
| 29 | Rusk Stays | 2,500 | character interior | The job, the office, the region, the offer. Four doors. | She takes the one that keeps her in the county. For the investigation, ostensibly. |
| 30 | Where It Began | 1,300 | coda | The 2019 file is reopened. It is now a homicide file. | The last line is the reopened determination, and the name on it is **hers.** |

**SOLVED — 2026-09-27, before chapter 1.** `research/clue-ledger-solve.md` records an
independent solve run against the published ledger only, without the answer key, the
`premise.md` §3 mechanism chain, or the chapter pulls. **Verdict: solvable**, with two
defects found and fixed: (1) Aylward was not cleanly excluded as instigator — fixed by
adding item 15 at ch 5, which excludes him and deepens the everyone-is-helpful theme;
(2) item 13 was the only clue failing first-read visibility — fixed by making the
pattern a stated observation at ch 14. Neither fix moved a chapter boundary.

**Premise 1 asserted solvability and never tested it. This one was tested.** The
residual caveat is in the solve file: the solve was run by the mind that wrote the
ledger, which is weaker than an outside reader. The real verdict is `/proof-panel` in
Phase 4.
