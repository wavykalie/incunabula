# ARCHIVED — Premise 1, "the documentary engine"

**Archived 2026-09-27 by human decision (`CONF-003`: CHANGE THE PREMISE).**
Not deleted. Read this before assuming the mechanism is gone.

## What this was

A county records clerk discovers a report the county says was never written is filed
in the wrong year. The man erased from the record 15 years ago is the one knocking
down the wall it is hidden in. The crime scene was a filing system; the sought object
was a paper record inside a condemned building; the clock was the wrecking date.
Theme as question: *what does a county owe the record it keeps, when the record is the
only thing in the room that cannot be intimidated?*

Dimension 7 = Solvability. The wound was Wren's own complicity in a 1996 records
certification that erased Evan Tallis.

## Why it was abandoned

The market research (`/scout`, `research/market-research.md`) falsified the
load-bearing commercial assumption:

- **The protagonist slot was already occupied.** Tom Baragwanath, *Paper Cage*
  (Knopf 2024) — a police-station records clerk, small town, buried institutional
  wrong. Ngaio Marsh Award finalist, 2025 Barry Award nominee. That is this engine,
  in print and award-nominated, since 2022.
- **The quiet middle of the market does not exist.** The CrimeReads 2025 best-of 20
  contains no quiet documentary procedural. Trade reporting describes a
  *bifurcation* between escape-reading and dark-reading, not a market with a
  middle.
- **Both comparable comps without series momentum were received disappointingly.**
  Tudor at "the 80% mark"; Doughty at 3.5/5, called a "ripping beach-read."

The user took the premise change rather than a repositioning. Recorded as
`CONF-003` in `CANON_LEDGER.yaml`.

## What survives, and should be reused

Not nothing. Three things carried into the next premise and should not be re-litigated:

1. **A working `content_boundaries` block.** The no-minors, off-page death, no-graphic-
   depiction, and no-pure-villain constraints are sound and genre-independent.
2. **A verified length contract.** 80,000–95,000, declared in `PROJECT_STATE.yaml` and
   parsed by `check-length.py` as `(80000, 95000, 'PROJECT_STATE.yaml')`. Enforced,
   not advisory. Keep it.
3. **A verified structural approach to chapter length.** The 30-chapter plan with CV
   35.7% sits inside the published-fiction band (33.4–45.1%) and was checked BEFORE
   drafting — which is the whole lesson from book 1, where the variance was only
   discovered in Phase 3. The *method* transfers even though the *premise* does not.

## What is genuinely lost

`premise.md` (873 words), `outline.md` (1,526 words), `CANON_LEDGER.yaml` (1,714
words), the 10-item fair-play clue ledger, and `CONF-001` (whether Caudle is
responsible-but-not-killer or a killer) — **a human decision, taken to the grave
unasked.** `CONF-001` is worth remembering: it was a good question, cleanly
escalated, and it died with the outline rather than being answered. Whoever rebuilds
this should expect a similar fork around chapter 24 and should try to resolve it
*before* the outline is built, not after.

## Note on the process failure, not just the premise

Two of six comps in the original set were **fabricated** — one invented outright, one
invented twice over. The premise was then built through three phases on top of a
market gap that had no evidence behind it. The premise was not unlucky. The
*ordering* was wrong: Phase 2 ran before Phase 1. Both failures are logged to
`incunabula/ERRATA.md` (now 35 entries) rather than quietly repaired here.
