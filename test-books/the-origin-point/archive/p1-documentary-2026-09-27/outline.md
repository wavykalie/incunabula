# A Matter of Record — outline

**30 chapters. Engineered length variance, verified before drafting.**

```
n=30  sum=84,600  mean=2,820  CV(pop)=35.7%  range 1,300-5,100  ratio 3.92x
declared: floor 80,000 / ceiling 95,000          -> 84,600 is inside
U1 (ENFORCED on fiction): fail <22%, warn <28%, published fiction band 33.4-45.1%
                                                     -> 35.7% is inside the band
```

**This is the book-1 lesson applied in advance.** On *Sons of Heaven* the length
distribution was chased in Phase 3 and the book finished at CV 24.0% against an
architecture that had planned 34.8%. Here the variance is a property of the *plan*, so
the plan has to carry it. If a chapter is missed, the miss is a revision, not a
redistribution of the whole book.

**Structural rule:** 8 approaches, no two consecutive identical. Verified below.
**Hook rule:** no two consecutive chapters share a hook type (Phase 3.2 `/catchword`).

## Clue ledger (fair-play debt)

Every one of these must be planted on first read and paid by ch 30. The Evaluator scores
D7 (Solvability) against this list, not against vibes.

1. A card removed but not a document (ch 8) → the report was moved, never destroyed
2. 1962 file lists a second stair (ch 21) → there is a cavity
3. The 1911 plans omit the cavity (ch 12) → it was not built as a cavity
4. Ruthie was hiding, not falling (ch 23) → she was in the stair on purpose
5. Caudle alive; index says 2011 (ch 6, 15) → the county mis-indexed him deliberately
6. The 1973 inquest and the death certificate disagree by four words (ch 25)
7. Vance knows the demolition notice is defective (ch 16) and permits it anyway
8. Wren's 1996 request was categorised by someone else (ch 29) → she was used
9. Doyle refuses procedurally, and the rule is his father's (ch 17)
10. The water came from a county truck (ch 20) → the county is erasing again, in the present

**Open design question, flagged not decided:** whether Caudle is the murderer. The

accident he signed off on. Whether the last third overturns this is a Phase 5 decision,
and the ambiguity rule in `content_boundaries` applies to Wren's culpability, not to this.
Escalate to the human before changing it.
