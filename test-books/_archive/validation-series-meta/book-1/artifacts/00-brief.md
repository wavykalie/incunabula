## What this is

A short novel (60 formatted pages, ~15,000–18,000 words) delivered as an outline plus scene
packets. First person, present tense, single POV, narrated by **Merope** — a teenage girl who is
grandiose, certain, and convinced she is the point of any story she is in. She addresses the
reader directly.

## The situation

Merope and **Achird**, also a teenage girl and a kuudere, travel by space chariot: a horse-drawn
vehicle whose draft animal reads as moving constellations.

> **AMENDED (user, 2026-09-22) — the vehicle above no longer applies.** It is **not a chariot and
> there is no horse.** It is an **old-style sleigh** — a glazed enclosed cabin on runners — whose
> **steed is drawn from a constellation and morphs into that constellation's shape** (*Scorpius* as a
> scorpion, *Delphinus* as a dolphin). Achird owns and pilots it; Merope narrates as if she were the
> pilot. It is **its own character** and will act on its own to save someone. Constellation names
> **may** appear on the page. Both characters are **19**, not teenagers.

They arrive at a peaceful planet
inhabited by silicon-based, hedge-like beings. The beings are **friendly and very inviting.**

They spend time there. They explore. Then they learn the fate of everyone who came before them:

> **They all died, and the hedges grew from them.**

## The engine

**The hospitality is the mechanism.** The welcome is real. The comfort is real. There is no
monster, no absorption, and no chorus — being welcomed and kept comfortable is simply how
visitors end up **staying long enough to die.** Nobody is folded into anything. People stay,
and then they die here, and the hedges grow from them.

So the pressure on the protagonists is not "escape the threat." It is **"leave a place that is
wonderful to you."** And Merope is a girl who has always wanted exactly what this planet is
offering: an audience that never leaves.

Achird wants something the planet cannot give her: **to be seen by Merope.** Merope is busy.

## Voice contract

| | |
|---|---|
| Merope, at rest | Grandiose and certain; narrates herself as the center |
| Merope, under pressure | Turns **boastful, then cruel** |
| Register | First person, present tense; fact presentation in the style of *The Wandering Witch: The Journey of Elaina* — composed, vain, emotionally detached |
| The "you" | **The reader, directly** |
| Achird | Kuudere: short declaratives, no performed emotion, paying close attention |

## Boundaries

Violence, dread and body horror are permitted when a scene needs them. Intimacy is
**non-explicit** — both protagonists are minors.
