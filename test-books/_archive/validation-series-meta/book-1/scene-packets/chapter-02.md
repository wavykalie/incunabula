# Scene Packet — Chapter 2 (title DEFERRED — working: *the welcome, from two sides*)

**Movement:** I — The welcome
**Structure:** parallel (two halves cut together)
**Target:** ~2,450 words
**When:** **day two.** The day after the arrival. (Fixed 2026-09-22 — 'the same day' was undefined and
ch. 3 needs a stable normal day count before time starts misbehaving. Do not put the day number on
the page; the reader should feel a settled routine, not a tally.)
**Theme in play:** being watched and being seen are not the same thing — first demonstration.

---

## Scenes

The chapter alternates between two halves of the same day. Cuts are clean and unmarked; the reader
should be able to tell which half they are in from the voice alone.

### A-half — Merope
- The beings surround her. They are delighted by her specifically and say so.
- She performs. She is good at this and enjoys it without embarrassment.
- The beings make room for her without being asked, repeatedly, all day.

### B-half — Achird
- She is welcomed **exactly as warmly** and is almost entirely unwatched, because Merope is standing
  in front of the attention.
- She is not excluded by the beings. They like her. That is the problem.
- **She takes measurements** — the interval between the light changing and the beings turning. She
  is the only one counting anything. It is meaningless and she does it anyway.
- She pauses and looks at things for slightly too long.

### S — the object (appears in both halves)
- Achird shows Merope the object from chapter 1.
- **Merope laughs at it, hands it back, and changes the subject** in a single casual sentence.
- Achird asks **one of the beings** what it is. The being answers **honestly, helpfully, and
  cheerfully** — and the answer means nothing to it. It is the first cold thing in the book.

---

## Required elements
- Both halves are **pleasant.** Alternation must not read as one warm half and one sad half.
- The two voices are distinguishable: Merope expansive and addressed outward; Achird short,
  declarative, precise.
- The beings' answer about the object is delivered in **one flat line with no emphasis, no pause
  before it, and no reaction after it.**
- Merope narrates over Achird at least twice, without malice and without noticing.
- The beings never withhold and never explain. They answer and move on.

## Voice notes
- Merope: chatty, quick, rhetorical questions, present-tense asides. No winking.
- The B-half is still **Merope's narration** — Achird has no POV. Achird's half is told by someone
  who is not quite looking at it, and the reader must notice what she does not.
- Emotional beats land in one flat sentence and are not dwelt on.

## Continuity constraints
- Object established in ch. 1; **no second object yet.**
- The beings' kindness is genuine in both halves.
- No being is ever evasive, cryptic, or reluctant. If one seems to be hiding something, rewrite it.
- Achird's measurements are consistent: same interval, same method, twice.

## Opening line
> Open mid-welcome, in Merope's voice, as though the reader has been standing there for an hour.

## Exit pull
- **Type:** wrong-footed.
- **Content:** nothing on this planet needs anything from them — not food, not space, not
  explanations. There is nothing for her to win here and she does not notice.

## Forbidden
- Making Achird sad, excluded, or pitied on a first read. She reads as **normal.**
- Merope being defensive about the object. She is light about it. She is not hiding yet.
- Any being acting as an oracle, a warn-er, or a keeper of secrets.
- Explaining what the object might mean.
