# Scene Packet — Chapter 3 (title DEFERRED — working: *she is happy here*)

**Movement:** I — The welcome
**Structure:** spiral (the same day three times, each pass deeper)
**Target:** ~2,450 words
**Theme in play:** a place that asks nothing does not take anything either — and she cannot tell the
difference yet.

---

## Scenes

The chapter is **the same day three times.** Each pass is slightly longer, slightly warmer, slightly
more comfortable. Time is not behaving; days are being lost. No one comments on it.

### Pass 1
- A morning that is warm, easy, and hers. She wakes rested. Nothing is asked of her.
- Achird finds **a shape in the growth that is not a plant**, and says so, flatly.

### Pass 2
- The day again, deeper. The beings are glad to see her *again* in exactly the same way.
- Merope narrates over Achird's warning from pass 1 — retells it as a quirk, a thing Achird does.
- Achird finds **another object** — the second — and this time asks her directly to come and look.
  **Merope comes, looks at it for as long as it takes to decide it is not worth looking at, and goes
  back to what she was doing.** She does not remember it an hour later.
  *(This beat is required, not optional: ch. 4 has her recognise this object on the shelf, so she has
  to have seen it. The dismissal is also what makes ch. 5's reversal cost her something — she looked
  and waved it away.)*

### Pass 3
- The deepest, easiest version. She is **happy** — genuinely, unironically happy.
- She stops counting her audience, because it is finally large enough that counting is unnecessary.
- Achird says her second warning, in one sentence. Merope narrates over it again, warmly, as though
  it were affection.

---

## Required elements
- Three passes, distinguishable, each **pleasanter** than the last.
- The chapter must be **the most enjoyable chapter in the book to read.** Smoothest prose.
- Both of Achird's warnings are **plain, correct, and unremarkable** — no drama, no edge, no
  argument. The reader should hear them clearly. Merope should not.
- The repeated day is never explained and never mentioned by anyone but Merope, casually.
- No threat of any kind appears.

## Voice notes
- This is the chapter where Merope's narration is at its most **charming.** She is not performing
  for the reader here; she is simply enjoying herself and telling you about it.
- Happiness must be reported, not analysed. No introspection about *why* she is happy.
- Emotional beats in one flat sentence.
- No winking, no irony, no self-awareness about the trap.

## Continuity constraints
- Objects found: one in ch. 1, one in this chapter. Achird's collection is growing off-page and
  should be visible in small ways (a pocket, a habit of stopping).
- **Both objects end up on the ch. 4 shelf, and Merope has seen both** — the first shown to her in
  ch. 2, this one in pass 2. `ENTITY_STATE.yaml` tracks them as ITM-001 and ITM-002.
- The **non-plant shape** Achird notices in pass 1 is never returned to in this chapter and never
  explained. It is not a monster and must not read as one.
- Time distortion stays soft and unremarked — days lost, no one counting.

## Opening line
> A plain statement about the morning, in the present, as though there is nothing unusual about any
> of this.

## Exit pull
- **Type:** question closed then reopened.
- **Content:** nothing is required of her — so nothing is happening to her either.

## Forbidden
- **Sag.** If this chapter sags or feels like filler, it has failed; it is the trap working and must
  be delightful.
- Any menace, omen, or wrongness the reader can name.
- Merope being ironic about her own happiness.
- Achird raising her voice, arguing, or showing frustration. She states things and stops.
- Explaining where the days went.
