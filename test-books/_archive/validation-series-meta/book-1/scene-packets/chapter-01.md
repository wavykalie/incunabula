# Scene Packet — Chapter 1 (title DEFERRED — working: *arrival*)

**Movement:** I — The welcome
**Structure:** chronological
**Target:** ~2,450 words
**Theme in play:** nothing is required of her yet — the whole chapter is the offer.

---

## Scenes

### S1 — aboard the sleigh, in transit, night
- The sleigh comes out of the dark: **an old-style enclosed cabin on runners, glazed**, travelling
  where there is no ground. Its steed is **a constellation that has taken a shape**. Tonight it is
  shaped from one constellation and the shape moves with the constellation's own movement.
- **Achird is flying it.** Merope narrates as though she were the one flying it, and does not
  mention Achird's hands once.
- **Merope addresses the reader for the first time** and introduces herself as the thing worth
  watching.
- Below them: a world that is not on any chart, and she says so without concern.

### S2 — descent and landing
- The light here is easy. Nothing about arriving is hard.
- Things grow in shapes that suggest they were **expecting guests.** She takes this as a compliment
  rather than a warning, and says exactly that.

### S3 — the clearing, morning
- **The beings come.** Not one, not a delegation: all of them at once, and they are **glad.**
  Sincerely glad. There is no ceremony and no caution.
- Merope is delighted, immediately and entirely at home.
- **Achird says almost nothing** and watches everything.

### S4 — evening, end of chapter
- Achird picks up **a small object** that does not belong here — not grown, not made here — and puts
  it away without saying anything about it.
- She says one flat sentence. Merope does not answer it, and describes the sunset instead.

---

## Required elements
- Opening line: *"I have been looked at before."*
- Direct address to the reader established and maintained.
- The steed is **a constellation wearing a shape** — never a horse, no shafts, nothing to harness.
- The beings' welcome reads as **unambiguously friendly.** No subtext of menace.
- Achird present throughout, speaking minimally, attending closely.
- Merope's want (to be watched) legible by the end without being stated.
- Achird's find happens **on the page**, quietly, and is not explained.

## Voice notes
- Grandiose, certain, self as centre. She narrates the arrival as though she arranged it.
- **Elaina register:** short paragraphs, flat reporting, present-tense asides, rhetorical questions.
- **She never winks.** Elaina's vanity is a joke she is in on; Merope's is sincere. No
  self-deprecation anywhere in this chapter.
- She does not explain the sleigh, the travel, or where they came from. She assumes you know.

## Continuity constraints
- **No planet name, no name for the beings.** Constellations may be named.
- **No dread, no threat, no ominous detail, no foreshadowing.** If the chapter feels eerie on a
  first read, it is wrong.
- Achird is flat and attentive — not cold, not sad, not mysterious.
- Nothing here has been left by anyone. Nothing on the page connects to anybody else. That belongs
  to chapters 4–5.

## Opening line
> *"I have been looked at before."*

## Exit pull
- **Type:** question opened, plus one unexplained object.
- **Content:** something here has been expecting visitors, and she cannot tell for how long.

## Forbidden
- Any competence cascade. She solves nothing and demonstrates no skill.
- Naming the planet or the beings.
- A horse, a chariot, a cart, or anything drawn between shafts.
- Explaining the magic, the travel, or their history.
- Making the object significant to Merope. **She has not seen it.**
