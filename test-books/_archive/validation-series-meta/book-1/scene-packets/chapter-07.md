# Scene Packet — Chapter 7 (title DEFERRED — working: *leaving, and the acknowledgement*)

**Movement:** III — The turn
**Structure:** chronological
**Target:** ~2,450 words
**Theme in play:** nothing stopped her, and nothing ever would have.

---

## Scenes

### S1 — the departure
- The sleigh leaves. **Achird flies it.** Merope narrates as though she does, exactly as in ch. 1 —
  the book should close on the same lie it opened on.
- The steed takes a new constellation's shape as it goes.
- **Nobody stops them. Nobody watches them leave.** The beings are glad to see them go.

### S2 — what leaving costs
- Nothing. That is the horror and it must not be dramatised.
- Merope does not announce why she left and does not know why. She does not examine it.
- The case for staying is still intact. Nothing was disproved. She simply went.

### S3 — the look
- On the way out, **she finally looks at Achird.** Properly, for the first time in the book.
- She realises Achird loves her. She does not say so, and cannot.
- She deflects. She is a tsundere and it shows. **The acknowledgment happens anyway** — small,
  sideways, unmistakable, and never named.

### S4 — quiet close
- Achird does not say it either. It is acknowledged between them and left there.
- End on a **quiet image**, not a punch and not a summary.

---

## Required elements
- The book's opening lie repeated at the close: Merope narrating Achird's work as her own.
- Leaving is **easy.** Nothing pursues, blocks, mourns or objects.
- The realisation about Achird is **Merope's**, on the page, without dialogue confirming it.
- The acknowledgement is real and unresolvable — not stated, not consummated, not deferred.
- **Nothing is lost. Nobody pays. Nobody is wounded.**
- Final beat is an image.

## Voice notes
- The narration should slow down here for the first time. Same voice, fewer performances.
- She rebuilds her composure **sentence by sentence, in front of the reader** — that is the visible
  movement of the chapter, and it should be legible without comment.
- No winking. The defence mechanism is sincere; she is not in on it.
- Emotional beats in one flat sentence.
- **The chapter should feel longer than it is.**

## Continuity constraints
- Achird has said exactly one sentence since ch. 5, and said it in ch. 6. **She says nothing in
  this chapter.** The realisation must come from Merope watching her, not from Achird saying
  anything.
- The beings are glad, uncomprehending, and not sad. No goodbye scene with a being.
- No being ever explained anything, so nothing needs resolving. Do not resolve anything.
- No **visitor's** body, no remains of a person, and no victim anywhere in the chapter. Remains of
  something that was not a person are permitted by the world rules; remains of someone who came
  here are not.
- Constellations may be named.

## Opening line
> Motion. Already leaving, mid-sentence, as though the chapter began before the choice.

## Exit pull / residue
- **Type:** emotion, not evaluation.
- **Content:** *She got what she wanted, it was smaller than she thought, and she is not sorry.*

## Forbidden
- Any being grief-stricken, angry, or turning hostile at their departure.
- A chase, a pursuit, or a last-minute danger. **Nothing threatens them, ever.** Note: the
  sleigh's decision on the way out *is* the saving act — staying is what kills her — and that is
  meaning, not a rescue scene. Do not write a rescue, and do not forbid the meaning either.
- Anyone dying, being wounded, or being left behind.
- Merope stating the theme, understanding herself, or explaining her choice.
- Stating Achird's love directly, in dialogue or in narration.
- A summary paragraph. **End on an image.**
