# Scene Packet — Chapter 6 (title DEFERRED — working: *the refusal*)

**Movement:** III — The turn
**Structure:** stream (Merope talking, at speed)
**Target:** ~2,450 words
**Theme in play:** she refuses the one thing that would ask anything of her.

---

## Scenes

This chapter is **Merope talking.** One continuous monologue broken only by what she will not let
Achird say. It is a stream — associative, accelerating, defensive, and finally cruel.

### Beat 1 — Achird says it
- **One sentence.** *We have to go.* Flat, complete, no preamble.
- Merope does not respond to it. She responds to something else entirely.

### Beat 2 — the performance
- She is at last, genuinely, the centre of everything. Nothing has ever asked her for anything.
- She argues. She lists. She argues about the objects, about the counting, about Achird's tone.
- Boastful. Then faster.

### Beat 3 — the cruelty
- She hurts Achird with **something accurate.** It is true. It has always been true and Achird has
  always known it, which is why she does not flinch.
- **Achird is not surprised.** That is the damage: she has heard it before.

### Beat 4 — nothing intervenes
- **No being appears. No being notices. Nothing changes.** The planet continues to be lovely.
- There is nothing to fight, nothing to flee, nobody to be afraid of. That is the whole point.

### Beat 5 — the sleigh
- The argument ends because **the sleigh moves on its own.** It has decided, and it will not be
  argued with.
- Achird goes to it. Merope follows, still talking.

---

## Required elements
- Achird's total dialogue in the chapter is **one sentence**, plus at most one word.
- The chapter runs long on Merope's voice and short on paragraphs. Shortest sentences in the book.
- **No confrontation with any being.** No being is on the page for Merope to be afraid of, or to
  argue with, or to be saved from.
- The cruelty is **true, accurate, and specific.** It must land as true for the reader as well.
- The sleigh acts of its own accord, against Merope, and nobody comments on it.

## Voice notes
- Stream: sentences that start and redirect, rhetorical questions unanswered, jokes with no
  audience. The reader should feel her talking **past** Achird.
- Keep her charming well into the chapter. The cruelty must arrive from charm, not replace it.
- Emotional beats in one flat sentence — including her cruellest line, which should be delivered
  plainly, not savoured.
- **No winking, and no self-awareness.** She is not ashamed while doing this.

## Continuity constraints
- Achird has not argued since ch. 5, and does not argue here. **Her one sentence is the whole of her speech in this chapter** — the largest moment in the book. Do not give her a second line.
- **No chase. No pursuit. No escalation. No threat.** (Decision C.)
- Merope still does not understand why she should leave, and never says a reason she believes.
- The beings remain glad. If one is on the page, it is happy and uncomprehending.

## Opening line
> Achird's one sentence, or Merope's voice cutting in before it finishes.

## Exit pull
- **Type:** decision forced, by the wrong party.
- **Content:** **Achird stops asking** — and the sleigh, which has been still the whole time, has
  already decided.

## Forbidden
- A being warning, resisting, blocking, pleading, or turning hostile.
- Any physical danger to anyone.
- Achird making a speech, crying, pleading, or expressing emotion in a performed way.
- Merope being punished, humiliated, or shown to know better.
- Merope stating the theme or understanding herself.
