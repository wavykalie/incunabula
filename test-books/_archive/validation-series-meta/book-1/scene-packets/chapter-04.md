# Scene Packet — Chapter 4 (title DEFERRED — working: *the belongings*)

**Movement:** II — Achird is not heard
**Structure:** fragmented
**Target:** ~2,450 words
**Theme in play:** the place keeps everything and returns nothing.

---

## Scenes

Verbatim fragments, uneven, unevenly sized. Do not smooth them into a scene.

### Fragment — she is led
- Achird takes her by the hand and walks her somewhere, which is the most Achird has ever done.
- Merope narrates the walk as a nice outing. She is pleased to be led by someone, for once.

### Fragment — a camp
- A place people lived. Not ruins — a place still standing, still usable, still neat.
- Nothing broken. Nothing buried. Nothing left in a hurry.

### Fragment — the shelf
- **A shelf of small things, laid out with care.** Objects from many different worlds, none of them
  from here, none of them recent, none of them claimed.
- Someone arranged them **the way you arrange things you are keeping for someone who is coming
  back.**

### Fragment — count
- Achird counts the objects. She has the number.
- She asks **the question nobody wants.** Merope answers it — wrongly, confidently, at length — and
  keeps talking.

### Fragment — the beings and the objects
- The beings handle the objects with total, cheerful familiarity, and with **no owner behind the
  familiarity.**
- Nobody is furtive. Nobody is sad. Nothing is concealed.

## Required elements
- **No visitor remains. No bodies, no bones, no trace of a body that was a person.** The absence is
  the entire horror and must never be named as an absence by any character. (Narrowed 2026-09-22:
  the rule protects the absence of *visitor* remains. Remains of something that was not a person are
  a different fact and are not forbidden. See `CANON_LEDGER.yaml → CONF-005`.)
- Merope's response is **pride, then pleasure.** It reads as: *people have always loved it here.*
- Achird asks her question **once**, plainly, and is not answered.
- No being explains anything, warns anyone, or changes tone.
- The shelf must be described as being **laid out with care** — do not use the word *kept*.

## Voice notes
- Fragments are literal. Short paragraphs, some one line. Uneven.
- Merope's narration stays light and chatty through all of this. **She is not unsettled.** Her wrong
  explanation must sound reasonable and generous, not stupid.
- Emotional beats in one flat sentence and not dwelt on.
- The single flat line that lands hardest should be **Merope's**, not a being's.

## Continuity constraints
- The objects from ch. 1 and ch. 3 (ITM-001, ITM-002) are **among** the things on the shelf.
  **Merope has seen both** — ch. 2 showed her the first, ch. 3 pass 2 the second — so she can
  recognise them. She should notice, and be pleased, and not understand why Achird has gone quiet.
- No bodies, anywhere, ever.
- No being is ever a host, an authority, or an informant. There is no spokesperson. (Decision C.)
- Nobody is pursuing them. Nothing has changed about the planet's behaviour toward them.

## Opening line
> A fragment — an object, or a hand, or a distance walked — not a scene-setting sentence.

## Exit pull
- **Type:** threat introduced, without a threat.
- **Content:** nobody has ever left, and **nobody is buried.**

## Forbidden
- Remains, bones, graves, monuments, or a marked place for the dead.
- Any character naming the situation, or asking *what happened to them* and receiving an answer.
- Making the beings sinister, evasive, or aware.
- Merope realising anything. She explains this away and means it.
- The word *kept* for the shelf; use *laid out with care.*
