# Scene Packet — Chapter 5 (title DEFERRED — working: *what the beings are*)

**Movement:** II — Achird is not heard
**Structure:** reverse (assembled backwards from a single fact)
**Target:** ~2,450 words
**Theme in play:** the planet answers the question by not having one.

---

## Scenes

Told backwards. Each step walks back from the fact toward its cause. The reader assembles it; no one
on the page ever states it.

### Step back — the welcome, again
- A being expressing unmistakable, delighted welcome **while standing where a person died.**
- Nothing in its manner changes.

### Step back — the habit
- A being performs a small habitual gesture that **came from someone.** It has no idea where the
  gesture came from. It is charming. It is the same gesture, made the same way, by more than one
  being.

### Step back — the growth
- The things growing here. The shapes that suggested they were expecting guests. They are not
  shaped for guests. They are shaped **out of what came before.**
- The non-plant shape Achird noticed in ch. 3 resolves here, quietly, as part of this.

### Step back — the fact
- Stated once, flatly, not by a character: **everyone who came here died here, and the beings grew
  from them.**
- Immediately after: **the beings have no idea.** They are not hiding it. They cannot lie. They are
  glad to see visitors because they have no memory of what visitors become.

### Forward — the explanation
- Merope finds the reading that lets her keep staying, and **states it to Achird, kindly, at
  length.** It is generous, warm, and wrong.
- **Achird does not argue.** She does not answer Merope's explanation at all — no rebuttal, no
  correction, no second attempt. **This is the last thing she says in the movement**, and she does
  not say it twice: she states the fact once and stops. Not mute. One sentence remains to her, in
  ch. 6. (Fixed 2026-09-22 — this line previously read "She says nothing at all," which contradicted
  both the sentence before it and ch. 6. `CRIT-001`.)

## Required elements
- The fact is **shown and assembled**, never stated by a character. The only explicit reading
  offered on the page is **Merope's wrong one.**
- The beings' ignorance is the chapter's turn and must land as **worse** than malice.
- The beings remain friendly, uncomplicated and glad, start to finish.
- Achird's silence is the final beat. **She stops arguing.** She states the fact once and stops; from here she says only what is necessary, and she says it once. She is not mute — one sentence remains to her, in ch. 6.
- Merope's wrong explanation sounds reasonable. A reader should be able to see why she'd believe it.

## Voice notes
- Reverse structure should be handled plainly — no italicised *earlier*, no gimmick.
- Merope's long explanation is the closest she comes all book to sincerity. Play it straight. It is
  a kindness with something wrong at the bottom of it.
- Emotional beats in one flat sentence.
- No winking, no irony. **She is not performing for the reader in this chapter.**

## Continuity constraints
- No being ever says what it is, what happened, or what will happen. (Decision C.)
- No host figure. The beings are a collective and none of them is singled out as an authority.
- The fact of the deaths must never be dramatised — no death scene, no victim, no name, and no
  visitor's remains shown. The growth may be shown incorporating **things** — a tool, a strap, a
  buckle, half-covered — so the mechanism is legible without a body ever being on the page. Remains
  of something that was not a person may appear; remains of a visitor may not. (`CONF-005`.)
- Achird stops **explaining and arguing** here and never argues again. She is not mute. Her line in
  this chapter is the last of movement II, she has **one more sentence** in the book (ch. 6), and
  after that she says nothing at all. Do not let this read as muteness, and do not give her a
  farewell line in ch. 7.

## Opening line
> A welcome. Warm, sincere, and addressed to her personally.

## Exit pull
- **Type:** irreversible knowledge, and a closed door.
- **Content:** Achird can no longer be heard, because she has stopped arguing. One sentence remains to her, and it is the largest thing she does in the book.

## Forbidden
- Any being explaining, warning, hinting, or going solemn.
- A death scene, a victim, remains, or a name for the dead.
- Merope admitting she might be wrong.
- Achird arguing, pleading, or having a scene. She stops.
- Stating the theme.
