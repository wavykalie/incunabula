# Adversarial audit — Book 2 (Phase 4)

**Run:** 2026-09-24, on the seven drafted chapters (15,077 words, prose gate 7/7 PASS, Phase 5.6
continuity gate cleared).
**Method:** read as a hostile stranger who has paid for this and is looking for a reason to stop. Book 1's
adversarial audit is the template; this one is run before the score, as the run brief requires.

**None of this is a defect list. It is a list of places where the book is exposed.**

---

## The strongest attack: the book has one idea and it is the title of the other book

Book 1's engine: *nothing is asked of you.* Book 2's engine: *you are asked for everything.* Book 1's
horror: staying is easy. **Book 2's horror: staying is easy, and leaving costs someone else.**

Both books end with the same structural event — the sleigh leaves and nothing stops it. The second time,
a hostile reader can say the series has one move, and the inversion is a coat of paint on it.

**Where the defence is and whether it holds.** The cost has moved. In Book 1, leaving cost nothing and that
was the horror. In Book 2, the cost is real, it lands on people who never asked Merope for anything, and
she does not pay it. That is a different book. **It holds, but it holds on one chapter's shoulders** —
ch.7's *"They were poorer"* paragraph is doing all the work of differentiating the two endings. If a
reader skims ch.7, the books collapse into each other.

---

## The exposures, worst first

### 1. The corrected rule did not reach the prose — ch.7 stops at the minimum

The author struck the pipeline's prohibition on directness (`CONF-011`) and the correction was written
into the canon, the foundation, the voice contract and the ch.7 beats. **The drafted chapter still
resolves on two words: *"I know."***

That may be exactly right. It is also **indistinguishable from the book that was obeying the old rule**,
because the same designer wrote both. A reader cannot tell "the prohibition was lifted" from "the
prohibition was honoured quietly."

- **Severity: this is the most important item in this file, and it is not a defect.**
- **Action taken:** raised, not fixed. Changing it would be the pipeline deciding how much directness the
  author wanted, which is precisely how `CONF-011` happened in the first place.
- **For the author:** if the intent behind striking the rule was that more should be said, this is the
  place it would go, and it is currently two words long.

### 2. Merope's agency is real but it is quiet

Movement II is her reacting: someone leaves, she is angry; she learns the pattern, she makes a speech; she
tests, and the tests do not land. Her clearest act in the whole book is the last one in ch.6. **A reader
who reads for plot may read movement II as a girl standing in a row being told things.**

**Defence:** the testing is designed to be legible without being named, and the ch.6 walk-off scene is
staged concretely. **It holds, but it is the book's thinnest load-bearing wall.**

### 3. The workers are a condition, not a cast

Four of them are individuated: the man with the two hands, the woman at the turn, the one checking tops,
the one who cannot remember. The rest are hands in rows. Book 1's beings were allowed to be a collective;
Book 2's workers are supposed to be **people who each chose the same thing**, and the manuscript does not
give enough of them enough of a face to carry that.

**Verdict: partially exposed.** The horror requires their contentment to be legible as many individual
lives, and at present it is legible as one mood.

### 4. The reveal is delivered in a single chapter and could read as information

Ch.5 assembles the pattern in reverse and *states* it — *"Everyone here came because somebody stopped
wanting them"* — which is the only sentence in the book where the narrator hands the reader a conclusion.
Book 1's version of this (ch.5, the beings grew from the dead) was assembled by the reader and never
stated. **Book 2 states it. That is a deliberate difference in method and a reader may find it less.**

**Defence:** the statement is followed immediately by Merope using it to elevate herself above the people
it is about, which makes the statement the setup for a character beat rather than an explanation. **Holds,
narrowly.**

### 5. The sleigh is nearly a prop

Book 1's sleigh was the one thing in the book that could act against the protagonist's narration.
Book 2 parks it at the edge and uses it twice, at each end. A reader who liked it is not served, and
`OBJ-001`'s card (`must_not: become convenient; be downgraded to a conveyance`) is not violated but is
also not exercised.

**Verdict: exposed, and accepted.** The book is about a place that needs everyone; a vehicle that needs no
one would have been a second engine, and the format reference is one conceit per installment.

### 6. There is nothing to be afraid of, on purpose, for seven chapters

No antagonist, no threat, no chase, no deadline. The tension map is a plateau with one spike. **A reader
trained on the first movement of Book 1 to expect dread to build will find Book 2 flatter for longer.**

**Defence:** the discomfort is available from ch.1 in the form of *nobody looks up*, and it is the same
discomfort throughout rather than a rising one. **This is the engine working as designed, and it is also
the book's biggest commercial exposure.**

### 7. Achird's largest moment requires the reader to make the leap

*"Then I'll take a part."* — five words, and everything depends on the reader connecting it to ch.5's
account of how the woman at the turn arrived. Merope makes the connection explicitly and fast. **A reader
who does not will experience ch.6 as a girl saying something flat about tomorrow.**

---

## What the hostile reader cannot get at

- **The voice.** Merope is unmistakable, she never winks, and the register does not slip once across
  15,000 words. No line in the book could be reassigned to Achird and survive.
- **The rule discipline.** Nothing is named, nobody explains, nobody threatens, and the book is
  recognisably the same series while being nothing like the same book.
- **The ending's honesty.** It does not punish Merope for wanting what she wanted, and it does not reward
  her either. The theme is never stated and cannot be extracted as a thesis without the reader doing it.
- **The correction held.** A rule the layer invented was caught, recorded and struck, and none of it
  leaked into the prose as a plot point.

---

## Gate

**Phase 4: PASSED — the book may be scored.** Seven exposures recorded, one of them (item 1) escalated to
the author rather than fixed, two accepted by design, four carried as craft exposure for any Phase 5 pass.

The most useful line in this file is the first one: **the two books share a move.** Whether that reads as
a series identity or as a rut is the single judgement a Phase 5 reading loop should be pointed at, and
Phase 5 has still never been activated, because its activation condition — a quality target set by the
author — has still never been met.
