# Market map — Book 2 (Phase 1)

**Method: desk analysis only. No live research was run.** This is the same standard Book 1's market map
met, and it is recorded here rather than assumed: `ASSUMPTIONS.md → B-4`. Nothing in this file is a data
claim. It is reasoning, and it is labelled as reasoning.

**Comps: none approved.** The author has never approved a comp title for this series. The three works in
`PROJECT_STATE.yaml → series_references` are **craft references** (what governs POV, tone, format), not
market comps, and must not be presented to a reader as comps.

---

## 1. What Book 2 is being sold as

The series contract does not change at the volume boundary (`../CANON_LEDGER.yaml → series.exists`):

| | Value |
|---|---|
| Form | short novel / serial installment, ~60 formatted pages |
| Aim | 15,000–18,000 words |
| Ceiling | 22,000 words (raised 2026-09-23, with the engine) |
| Chapters | 7 |
| Market | English-language e-book, serial-friendly |

**The one thing that changes:** Book 1 was a single-mechanism book — one planet, one engine, four
characters. Book 2 has a **working world**, which is why the ceiling moved. If Book 2 runs past 22,000
the shape needs re-cutting, not the ceiling raising again (`ASSUMPTIONS.md → A-2`).

## 2. The honest position on the market

An unnamed, untitled second installment of a series with no comps and no live research has **no market
claim behind it at all**, and the useful thing this file can do is say so plainly rather than manufacture
one.

What it *can* say:

- **Serial positioning is structural, not promotional.** The books are sequential and standalone-ish
  installments around one pair of characters. That is a real packaging shape (episodic, one conceit per
  installment, low per-unit commitment) and it is the shape the format reference governs
  (`PROJECT_STATE.yaml → series_references → C`).
- **Book 2 is an inversion book.** Its engine is the structural opposite of Book 1's. For a reader who
  read Book 1, that inversion is the promise. For a reader who did not, it makes Book 2 the worse
  starting point — which is a **packaging** decision, not a drafting one, and is flagged for Phase 7.
- **The naming rule costs discoverability and is not negotiable.** `R-008`/`book-1 R-008` forbids naming
  anything on the planet. Book 1's scorecard already docked Worldbuilding to 7.0 for exactly this
  (`../book-1/artifacts/06-scorecard.md`). Book 2 inherits the cost and adds a second unnamed world.
  This is the single largest commercial tax in the series and it is a deliberate, author-set one.

**Nothing.** No change to length, chapter count, audience, or positioning follows from this analysis.

One assumption is re-flagged and left open rather than resolved:

- `ASSUMPTIONS.md → A-5` — Book 1's four reader personas are inherited as Book 2's starting audience.

  than in Book 1: the dread channel is different and there are no objects to count. Recalibration is
