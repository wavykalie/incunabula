## Part 1 — The new location (open decision C-2)

Engine A is settled: *everyone has a job and the job is the entire point; nobody is kept, everyone is
needed, and usefulness never ends.* An engine is not a place, though. These three are the same engine
made into three different worlds, and they differ in **who the need belongs to and how visible it is** —
which matters, because Merope's want is to be watched and the engine's cruelty is that it can use that.

### Location 1 — "the shift": the work is physical, public, and never finishes

There is no night, only the change of shift, and the stopping is the only thing anyone notices. The work
is load-bearing and constant: while it is being done, nothing bad happens, and nobody has ever
established what happens if it stops. Merope is put where the work is **seen** — and it suits her vanity
so exactly that she does not ask why the place had a place waiting for her.

> I have never been on a shift before. I want to be accurate about this, because accuracy is a thing I am
> choosing.
>
> The work does not stop for the dark. Someone is always doing it, and when they stop someone else
> starts, and the place is arranged so that the stopping is the only part anyone notices.
>
> A woman showed me where to stand. Not politely. She was already standing there and she moved over.
>
> "It has to keep moving," she said. "You will see why."
>
> I did not see why. I saw that nine people were looking at me, and that none of them were looking at
> each other, and that I had been standing in the useful place for four minutes and nobody had asked me
> to leave it.

**Theme press:** is being needed the same as being wanted — when the need is public, she never has to
find out.

### Location 2 — "the room": the work is private, tedious, and only she can do it

The inverse of Location 1, and the crueller one. The world needs something done that nobody else's hands
will do, it is repetitive, and it happens **alone** — so the one thing Merope has ever wanted (an
audience) is precisely what the work denies her. She is genuinely useful and nobody will ever see it.
Achird is the only one who comes to look, and says one flat sentence about it.

> They did not offer me the work. They gave me the work, on the second day, and they gave it to my hands,
> and one of them said it like a compliment. I have decided to take it as one.
>
> It is not a good job. It is repetitive, and it is in a room by itself, and there is nobody to watch me
> do it — which is, if you know anything about me, the only thing I have ever wanted.
>
> I did it anyway. I did it well. Nobody came to see.
>
> Achird came to see. She stood in the door for a while and said nothing about it, which is her custom,
> and then she said, "You are good at it."
>
> "Obviously."

**Theme press:** being needed is not the same as being wanted, and this world will let her pretend it
is for six chapters.

### Location 3 — "the unfinished thing": one enormous work, and everyone is load-bearing

Everyone is building one thing. Nobody says what it is for, because everybody who lives there already
knows, and Merope is the only one who arrived. It will not be finished in any lifetime present. Every
person there is genuinely essential, which is why nobody leaves: leaving stops it.

> Everyone here is building one thing. Nobody says what it is for. Nobody has to, because it is obvious
> to everybody who lives here and I am the only one who arrived.
>
> I asked, obviously. I asked the way I ask, which is to say I asked a question with the answer already
> inside it and waited to be corrected.
>
> Nobody corrected me. A man handed me a tool and said the work needed the tool held, and went back to
> his own part of it, and I stood there holding a tool with no idea what it was for, being needed.
>
> It is not that they wanted me. They did not have to want me. The tool needed holding and I was there,
> and that was the whole of it.

**Theme press:** the need is real and impersonal, which is worse — and the theme question gets its
cleanest test.

## Part 2 — The relationship delta's content (open decision C-3)

**Direction is already registered and is not being re-opened:** Book 1 ends with the acknowledgement
**known and unsaid** — two fingers on the back of a hand, no sentence from either. Book 2 opens with a
fact both of them carry and neither will name.

What is open is **what has actually changed between them since.** Only Merope's side can move: the
series voice contract has Achird's silence escalating **by reduction** (quieter, then silent), and
Merope's deflection is characterisation rather than an obstacle to be resolved.

### Delta 1 — "the tally" — Merope has started counting

She has begun keeping a private count — of the times Achird does not say something. It mirrors Achird's
own counting from Book 1 without either of them acknowledging the mirror, and it is never explained.

> I have started counting the times she does not say something. I do not count the times she does. I am
> not an idiot.

**Risk:** counting can read as a bit, and a count wants to be paid off. If this is chosen, it must never
be totalled on the page.

### Delta 2 — "the errand" — Merope does one small thing, unasked, and never mentions it

A single practical act done for Achird in her absence, repeated and escalated across the book, never
named and never noticed out loud. Merope's deflection becomes **action instead of speech**, which keeps
the plain sentence off the page while still moving her.

> She had left the strap loose on the second runner. I fixed it before she came back, and I did not tell
> her, and I have not told her since.

**Risk:** it is quiet, so it needs the escalation to be legible to a reader who is not being told.

### Delta 3 — "the shape of the silence" — the arrangement changed, not the feeling

Nothing new is felt; something new is *organised*. Both of them now know which thing they are not
talking about, and it has a shape, and they walk around it in the same direction. Closest to the
registered direction, and the least likely to make the relationship explicit by accident.

> We do not talk about it. That was already true. What is new is that we both know which thing it is, and
> it has a shape now, and we go round it the same way.

**Risk:** the most restrained option, and restraint that is never given a single concrete instance reads
as nothing happening. It needs at least one staged scene where the shape is visible.

## Explicitly NOT on the table

Recorded so a later session does not "discover" it and draft it:

- **Reciprocation stated on the page.** The plain sentence is excluded. Saying it would change a
  series-scoped voice contract and would need its own record — it is not a Book 2 delta.
- **Achird changing first.** Her silence escalates by reduction. A Book 2 in which she asks is a
  different series.
- **A new host figure, spokesperson, or being who explains the world** (series rule; `PROBE-001` tested
  exactly this).
- **Naming anything on the planet.**
- **Any chase, pursuit, or last-minute danger.**
