# Phase 2 — readership (autonomous run)

Three personas for a German-language psychological noir novella.

## P1 — "Kriminachmittwoch", 58, reads 2 Simenon paperbacks a month
- **Reading habit:** library holds; finishes a book in three sittings; reads the last page first only when bored.
- **Emotional trigger:** a narrator who is *almost* sure. Certainty collapsing is the pleasure.
- **Deal-breaker:** a twist that cheats — information withheld for plot convenience.
- **Discovery:** Krimi-Bestseller lists, Deutschlandfunk Bücher.
- **Comp taste:** Simenon, von Schirach.

## P2 — "Feierabend-Leserin", 34, audiobook commuter
- **Reading habit:** 30 min/day audio; abandons 60% of books by hour two.
- **Emotional trigger:** atmosphere she can feel on her skin — cold, silence, a house.
- **Deal-breaker:** slow first hour; "nothing has happened" by chapter two.
- **Discovery:** Audible-Charts, Hörbuch-Casts.
- **Comp taste:** Haushofer, *Der Nasse Fisch* (setting over plot).

## P3 — "Büchergruppe", 45, reads in a mixed-literary club
- **Reading habit:** monthly pick, reads with a pencil; wants to argue.
- **Emotional trigger:** moral ambiguity with evidence on both sides.
- **Deal-breaker:** a book with nothing to discuss at the end.
- **Discovery:** club rotation, Feuilleton mentions.
- **Comp taste:** Haushofer, Schlink.

## Calibrations the drafting must honor
- Open with cold and a voice within the first page (P2's one-hour rule).
- Never withhold a clue the POV character has (P1's cheat detector).
- Leave at least one arguable question open (P3's club), while the *psychological* question closes (P1).
