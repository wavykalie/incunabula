# Phase 4 — voice matrix (autonomous run)

## Book-level narrative register
Close third, past tense, German literary-crimi register: medium-short sentences, precise-professional vocabulary (her stenographer's diction), imagery limited to **cold / paper / sound** — one image per sensation, no two images for one feeling. Narration distrusts adjectives about people; it trusts procedure.

## Voice cards

### Elisabeth (POV)
- **diction:** bureaucratic exactness ("um 14:07 Uhr", not "nachmittags"); time-stamps speech habitually.
- **rhythm:** long grammatical sentences that end in short verdicts.
- **under pressure:** her syntax goes *more* exact, never less; she lists.
- **break mode:** when the record fails her she speaks in sentence fragments — the only place she is ever fragmentary.
- **never:** she never uses slang and never uses "wir".

### Kessler
- **diction:** plain, working, evasive; answers the adjacent question.
- **rhythm:** short clauses with "vielleicht" for ballast.
- **under pressure:** he becomes formally polite — "Sie", full titles.
- **break mode:** one full uninterrupted confession-length paragraph (ch6) — his only long speech in the book.
- **never:** he never says his brother's name aloud.

### Radke
- **diction:** domestic idioms, weather vocabulary, humming.
- **under pressure:** she narrates chores like a checklist.
- **break mode:** none — she does not break; the house does.

## Anti-pattern budgets
- em-dash: ≤1 per 1,000 words (humanizer discipline).
- simile: ≤1 per chapter, must be functional (cold / paper / sound only).
- "as if": max 3 in the manuscript.

## Benchmark samples (blind-sort testable)
- **Elisabeth:** "Der Mann sagte, er habe sie um Viertel nach elf gefunden. Das war gelogen, aber es war eine saubere Lüge, und ich habe sie aufgeschrieben wie jede andere."
- **Kessler:** "Vielleicht war er müde. Vielleicht war es das Wetter. Sie waren damals auch nicht da, gnädige Frau."
- **Radke:** "Die Leitung ist im Winter tot, das weiß man. Ich stell' den Kessel auf zwei, dann reicht's bis morgens."
