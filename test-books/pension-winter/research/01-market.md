# Phase 1.5 — market study (autonomous run, seed 779398044)

Rolled frame: German · psychological thriller · noir · short novella (15,000–25,000 words).

## Comps
- **Simenon, romans durs** (e.g. *Die Flucht des Mr. Harley* / *L'Homme qui regardait passer les trains*) — the ceiling comp: psychological crime with no investigation to speak of, guilt as weather. Simenon sells steadily in German translation.
- **Marlen Haushofer, *Die Wand*** — the isolation comp: one mind under house arrest, the world shrinking. Canonical in German-language psychological fiction.
- **Ferdinand von Schirach, *Schuld***-cycle — the procedural-minimal comp: short chapters, flat exact prose, the legal record as a source of dread.

Comps are from the run's knowledge, not live market data — flagged UNVERIFIED for the author.

## Genre conventions (psychological thriller, German market)
- Sub-200k characters is normal for the crime/thriller pocket; a 20k novella is a *Kurzroman*, sold as ebook single and audio.
- Past tense literary narration is standard; present tense reads experimental.
- The "thriller" promise in German channels is *Erkenntnis* (recognition), not chase. A wrong-memory plot fits.
- Ending may be dark and unresolved if the *psychological* question closes.
