## Anti-inflation statement

The floor is a 7.0 and it is reported as a 7.0. No dimension was awarded 8.5+ because the
evidence does not support it, and the protocol's benchmark rule (a floor above 8.0 would
be the highest among the project's reference bestsellers) makes such a claim implausible
for a first draft scored by its own author. Three chapters' scores were *lower* after the
Devil's Audit than the writer's self-report implied.
