# Upstream signals

Written by the Colophon (Phase 7) for the Evaluator and the orchestrator. This is the
feedback loop that lets packaging send problems back to structure.

## Signals raised

### SIGNAL-1 · PREMISE CLARITY — **medium**

The packaging revealed a real problem the chapter evaluations did not see. The book's
premise ("eleven rulers paired with eleven theories of rule") is crisp in the
*description* but invisible in the *reading experience* for the first two chapters,
because a reader who does not already know the structure reads Chapters 1 and 2 as
conventional reign narratives.

**Recommended action:** a one-paragraph note at the front of the book — three sentences,
not a preface — that states the pairing structure. This is a *paratext* fix, not a
manuscript fix, and it is the cheapest high-value change in the whole run.

### SIGNAL-2 · STRUCTURAL SUSPENSE — **low**

The description promises "eleven verdicts on which of them held". The manuscript does not
render verdicts in the advertising sense — it renders *failures* — and a reader primed
for verdicts may feel the book is withholding. This is a marketing-register mismatch, not
a manuscript defect. **Recommended action:** change the copy, not the book.

### SIGNAL-3 · SCALE — **medium**

The manuscript is 28,263 words. The query letter describes it honestly and notes it would
be built to full trade length before submission. That is not a defect, but it is a
constraint on the packaging: the book cannot be sold on length and must be sold on the
argument and the voice.

## Signals NOT raised

- **No premise-clarity problem at the chapter level.** Each chapter's argument is legible
  in isolation.
- **No structural-suspense problem within the manuscript.** Chapter hooks and pacing are
  strong at chapter granularity; the sag is a middle-third aggregate, already logged in
  the full-manuscript evaluation.

## Effect on the run

Signals 1 and 2 are **paratext**. Neither triggers a re-evaluation of the manuscript, and
neither changes the scorecard. Signal 3 is a packaging constraint and is recorded in the
Colophon.

Under the post-packaging gate, a PREMISE CLARITY signal at *high* severity would require
a targeted macro-structure re-evaluation. This one is **medium** and is discharged by a
front note.
