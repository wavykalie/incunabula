# Quoin — Chapter 11

Skill: `quoin`. 6 of 8 operations applied.

1. **Cut self-explaining similes.** A draft called the examination "a funnel"; cut. The chapter's own list of mechanical details is more persuasive than any metaphor.
2. **Irrelevant thought injected.** The candidate memorising the *Great Learning* at six, and the family fortune turning on it, are kept as a domestic detail with no argument attached.
3. **Emotional control broken.** "An essay about a book" is the narrator being unfair and knowing it.
4. **Precision deflated.** The six-hundred-year span of Zhu Xi's orthodoxy is given as "roughly" and its start date as a century, not a year.
5. **Ugly sentence added.** The closing list of names and one-line failures is deliberately rhythmless, a ledger of men rather than a crescendo.
6. **Predictable paragraph removed.** A final paragraph that answered the book's question ("and so the answer is that legitimacy is always provisional…") was cut. The question is left open.
7. **Clean dialogue roughed.** N/A.
8. **Thematic echo unsettled.** The chapter deliberately does *not* resolve into a verdict. The closing move — address the reader directly, once, at the very end — is the largest structural risk in the book and was kept.

**Anchor preserved:** Zhu Xi's commentary on the test everyone must pass.
