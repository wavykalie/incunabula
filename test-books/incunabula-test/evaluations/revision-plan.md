## Structural

**R-001 · Ch 5, the encyclopedic stretch.**
*Evidence:* `eval-chapter-05.md` — the Wang Bi / Seven Sages paragraph; the Pure
Conversation section is the slowest passage in the book.
*Intervention:* cut the Wang Bi / Seven Sages paragraph by half. Let the elite's
abstention be the point rather than its bibliography.
*Risk:* the chapter loses its only account of the intellectual shift into the period of
division, which Chapter 6 assumes.
*Verify:* Ch 5 still sets up Ch 6's arrival without it.

**R-002 · Ch 11, the machinery paragraph.**
*Evidence:* `eval-chapter-11.md` and `08-devils-audit.md` §4.
*Intervention:* split the sealed-papers / locked-examiners paragraph so that the
mechanics alternate with the consequence, rather than arriving as a block.
*Risk:* low. Purely presentational.

## Connective

**R-003 · Ch 2, campaign compression.**
*Evidence:* `eval-chapter-02.md`.
*Intervention:* date two or three of the named engagements at first mention so a reader
who does not know the Chu–Han war can follow the alternation.

**R-004 · Ch 10, caveat placement.**
*Evidence:* `eval-chapter-10.md`.
*Intervention:* move the register-versus-body-count caveat *before* the figures, so a
skimming reader cannot take away the inflated number.

**R-005 · Ch 3, Wudi's thinness.**
*Evidence:* `eval-chapter-03.md`.
*Intervention:* one paragraph on what an examined official actually did, so the emperor's
achievement is felt before it is spent.

**R-006 · Ch 7, the cost embodied.**
*Evidence:* `eval-chapter-07.md`.
*Intervention:* one paragraph on the conscript's experience, drawn only from what the
sources record about the levy, without inventing a biography. *This is the highest-risk
ticket in the plan* — it is one step from the invented interiority the book forbids.

## Prose

**R-007 · Ch 8, one paragraph too many.**
*Evidence:* `eval-chapter-08.md`.
*Intervention:* cut the penultimate paragraph. Let the mirror land and stop.

**R-008 · Ch 1, Qin Shi Huang as a decision-maker.**
*Evidence:* `eval-chapter-01.md`.
*Intervention:* one short passage in which the emperor is seen deciding, not described as
an algorithm.

## Factual

**None outstanding.** The three factual errors found by the Phase 5.6 audit (CONT-101,
CONT-102, CONT-201) were arithmetic, were found and fixed, and were re-scanned.
