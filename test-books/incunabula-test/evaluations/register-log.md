"dialogue" is reported speech and document quotation, so this pass ran the Blind Sort in
its nonfiction form (`voice-matrix.md`: *cover the attribution — can the reader tell
which kind of document they are in?*).

## Voice bleeding found and fixed

1. **Ch 2, initial draft.** Liu Bang's staged speech had picked up the narrator's
   essayistic rhythm (long, balanced, ironic). Rewritten shorter and flatter — a man who
   apologises rather than a man who argues.
2. **Ch 6, initial draft.** The emperor's edicts had drifted toward the narrator's
   vocabulary. Restored to formulaic, impersonal, ritual register.
3. **Ch 9, initial draft.** The hostile record's accusations were being summarised in
   neutral narration, which laundered them. Rewritten so the hostility is audible.

## Ratio check

Reported speech / document quotation runs at roughly 12–18% of each chapter's text, and
no chapter exceeds 25%. The book is narration-first by design; the documents are
evidence, not dialogue.

## Result

All speaking figures pass the Blind Sort. No invented quotation anywhere (rule F-006).
No narrative prose was rewritten by this pass.
