## Assumptions that are load-bearing

- **A-13.** If the user expected original research, the book's authority claims change
  completely. This is the single most consequential inference.
- **A-05.** Where the book stops is a real editorial choice. Ending at the Song is a
  decision, not a neutral survey boundary.
- **A-16 (unresolved).** Whether the author wants the narrator to include themselves.
  The run proceeds with a third-person narrator and flags this rather than deciding.
