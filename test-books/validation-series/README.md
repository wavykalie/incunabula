# validation-series — v2 is live, v1 is archived

Two books, two builds. They are not the same manuscript and should not be confused.

| | chapters | words | where |
|---|---|---|---|
| **v2 — live** | 11 per book | 15,377 + 14,962 | `book-1/`, `book-2/` |
| **v1 — archived** | 7 per book | 15,458 + 15,079 | `_archive/validation-series-meta/book-*/manuscript/chapters/` |

**The live books are the eleven-chapter ones.** They were re-chaptered to break the uniform
lengths v1 had, and they are the version the current framework governs. Their full write-up,
including what the rewrite did *not* prove, is in `REWRITE.md`.

**The seven-chapter build is not lost.** It is in the archive, and it was recovered from the v1
EPUBs after the re-chaptering overwrote the chapter files in place. How that recovery was verified
is written up in `_archive/validation-series-meta/V1-MANUSCRIPT-RECOVERY.md`.

v1 is kept because it is the evidence for the uniformity finding the framework was written
against — seven chapters at a CV near 6% and 11%, the machine-shaped output the pipeline used to
produce by default.
